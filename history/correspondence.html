Historical · institutional witness

The Correspondence Archive, 2023–2026

eight distinct messages (forty-four public; four withheld, checksums published) preserving the framework's contact with the institutional record, 2023–2026: a Nature submission four months before the manuscript was finished; the arXiv account created on the day it was; three submissions and three moderation declines — one containing the question that names the whole episode; the endorsement chain two professors answered; the Foundations of Physics and Synthese decisions; the Quantum exchange stating the gate cascade in the journal's own words; the preprint-server declines; and the graduate applications that ended with Brown. Nearly every institutional message carries a DKIM signature, making it cryptographically self-authenticating from its raw bytes.

Situating note (September 2026). The record below closes before the framework’s formalization passes of August–September 2026 and before the author’s entry into graduate study in physics. Every submission it preserves was made with the framework’s ontology already complete (May 8, 2023) and its formal apparatus incomplete; the current long paper states which parts were later proved and which remain posited, and its closing ledger is the reference against which any earlier reviewer’s objection can now be checked.

The question
“Do you have a conventional publication record? In what field?” arXiv moderation, MOD-25133 · May 25, 2023 · DKIM-signed

Asked of a manuscript that — as the Marked Paper documents line by line — already contained the framework's complete load-bearing architecture. The filter asked about the author because it could not read the dialect the content arrived in. This page preserves the exchange not as grievance but as evidence: a dated, third-party record that the work existed, was finished, and was walked to the front door in May 2023 — and a priced instance of what credential proxies cost when the false negative is real.

“I have contacted 17 potential reviewers…” Synthese, editor's decision on SYNT-D-25-00828R1 · July 31, 2025 · DKIM-signed

An editor who declines a desk rejection seventeen times is an editor fighting for a paper that had no home field — reviewers accept what they feel competent to judge, and a manuscript spanning physics, the metaphysics of time, and an original formalism belonged to no one's competence. The archive preserves the full thread, revision round included.

The archive

The canonical evidentiary form is the zip of unaltered .eml originals — full headers, signatures intact. Public edition revised August 2026: four originals are withheld because their raw bytes identify private individuals — each withheld message's SHA-256 remains published in the manifest as a commitment, verifiable privately on request — and one byte-duplicate entry was removed, leaving forty-eight distinct messages, forty-four of them public. Every per-message checksum is unchanged from the July 2026 manifest. The .mbox is the same forty-four public messages as a single standard-format file. The originals are never re-saved, forwarded, or “cleaned up”: any byte change invalidates the signatures that make them self-authenticating.
