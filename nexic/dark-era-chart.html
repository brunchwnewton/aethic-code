The Dark Era

Weight vs. combat power for theropod dinosaurs and mammals, fitted via median regression. The gap between them is what our ancestors lived inside.

At our ancestor's size (100 g), a theropod has a 12× advantage.

How this works: Each dot is an animal with a known body mass and a crowd-sourced combat score (Bradley-Terry model, ~4,500 matchups, anatomy-corrected, Lion = 100). Median regression fits a power law to each group, robust to outlier votes. Mammals below 1 kg are excluded from the regression due to limited cross-scale calibration in the matchup graph.

What the gap means: The vertical distance between the curves is the anatomy advantage. A theropod and a mammal of equal mass are not equal fighters. The theropod's body plan gives it a built-in multiplier that mass cannot overcome. This gap is largest at the smallest sizes, exactly where our Mesozoic ancestors lived.
