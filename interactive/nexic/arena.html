What is measured

The scores are Bradley–Terry strengths fitted to public votes on animal matchups, African Lion = 100. A bout's odds are the vote share Sfighters / (Sfighters + Sanimal). The untrained man, the spear's premium and the way groups add up are read from those votes. The anatomy correction multiplies a dinosaur's crowd score by the paper's factor.

What is judgment

Skill, armour, the weapons other than spear and club, and how much of a duellist's skill carries over against an animal are estimates with wide bands, set in step 8 of the pipeline. Tick the uncertainty box and each bout draws the fighter, the animal and the group exponent from their bands.

What is staging

The winner of each bout is drawn with the model's share, and the exchanges are then choreographed to that result: a favoured side usually wins cleanly, an unfavoured one narrowly. The fighters count as beaten when half of them are down. Who is struck, and how many fall, come from that staging and not from data. A vote share is not a casualty forecast.
