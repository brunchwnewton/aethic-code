What is measured

The scores are Bradley–Terry strengths fitted to public votes on animal matchups, African Lion = 100. A bout's odds are the vote share Sone side / (Sone side + Sthe other). The untrained man, the spear's premium and the way groups add up are read from those votes. A dinosaur's conventional belief score is the one the crowd voted; its accurate score is that one multiplied by the paper's anatomy factor.

What is judgment

Skill, armour, the weapons other than spear and club, and how much of a duellist's skill carries over against an animal are estimates with wide bands, set in step 8 of the pipeline. Tick the uncertainty box and each bout draws its odds from those bands.

Bows and guns do not fit one number, because range changes everything. Those fights are simulated shot by shot from stated rates of fire, hit chances and stopping energies, starting as far apart as you set, and their odds are counted from the simulation. Step 8 still gives each firearm a rough place on the scale; the simulation does not use it.

ROTG Titans and Alaarn are a fantasy race from the story ROTG (nothing to do with Attack on Titan). Every fighter here is a training, arms and armour, and the race beside it is the body underneath: a human's, a Titan's (about twice the mass, a third taller, and built for it) or an Alaarn's (the Neanderthals of the Titans, a little shorter and much thicker). So a Titan legionary, or a Titan gun crew, is the same class on another body: more mass, more reach and one guessed premium for build, and behind a gun far less of each. The Adamantine Guard, the Wolfpacks and the Ascendent, last in the lists, are Titan training schools. On horseback: a tick in the row of any human armed for hand-to-hand (no horse carries a Titan or an Alaarn; nobody here shoots from the saddle). The cavalry classes come with it ticked. Nothing on the scale is mounted, so this is judgment: against animals the rider and the horse count as two fighting together, so the horse's own strength is in the pair's, and against people the saddle is worth about 4.5 to 1 over the same man on foot. On the sand they are two bodies. When the pair is beaten one of them is down, the horse six times in ten, and the other fights on: the man on foot, or the horse on its own strength. The Roman and Greek armies form up as they did: the legion in three lines behind a screen of velites, the phalanx in one deep line behind its peltasts with the picked men on the right, and the horse on the wings. A Character, where the first box of a row offers one, is a named figure of that story, with a strength and rules of his own in place of a class and a race.

Dinosaur and Mammal, in the first box of a row, are shorthand. "Dinosaur" holds every sauropsid on the page, which here means the dinosaurs, the birds (dinosaurs too, as it happens), one pterosaur and one giant lizard; "Mammal" holds every synapsid, the Permian ones included, which were not mammals yet. And Megalania, a lizard, is filed under Dinosaur with the terror bird. Pedants may read the two words as sauropsid and synapsid.

How a bout is fought

The winner is drawn first, with exactly the model's share, so the long-run tally is the Bradley–Terry share by construction. The fight you watch is then drawn from a fight model until it ends that way. In that model a hit takes (attacker's strength ÷ victim's strength)0.4 of the victim's staying power, a third between equals, so a T. rex downs a man in one bite and a man needs dozens of thrusts. None of this applies once a bow or a gun is on the sand: those fights are simulated outright. Who lands each exchange is solved so that one against one reproduces the share, and one tilt per side does the same for groups. A side is beaten when its last fighter is down. The 0.4 and the third are staging choices; they shape the fight and not the odds.

Mixed sides, three sides

Different kinds on one side add as (Σ s1/k)k, which gives nk for n alike. With three sides each wins in proportion to its strength, the multi-way form of Bradley–Terry; the votes are all one against one, so that is an extension. When everyone on the sand is human, the odds are real odds from step 8's duel ratings and no longer vote shares.
