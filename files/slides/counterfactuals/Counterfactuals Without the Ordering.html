Only the dual-peg operator affirms both premises and rejects the conclusion.
For one identifiable reason: it clears the support of a reopened conjunct. Every other row keeps s.
Reopening is not settling.
Settling, as an intervention, keeps the causes standing and validates Cautious Monotony (composition). Reopening keeps every law and gives up the particular grounds. Two questions, two operations.

Imaging consumes the ordering; the grade does not.
Lewis's transport plan is the ordering applied pointwise; here mass follows dependency and validity through the blank intermediate. Verdicts are never entries, so the triviality results find no update dynamics to exploit.
The classic cases, as declared structure and scope
Nixon's button
The button-to-launch law lives in the constraint layer, never retracted. The grim verdict needs no tuned weights, and no miracle inserts the antecedent.
Backtracking
Reopen narrowly and the past stays fixed; let the clearing run into the antecedent's declared grounds and the backtracking reading appears. Two scope settings, no hidden metric.
Morgenbesser's coin
Nothing wires the toss to the bet, so the heads entry stands in every pocket: "had you bet, you would have won" at grade one, at every setting.
The streetlight
Evidential-minimal: infeasible, since "proceeded" is protected and excludes red under the law. Unidirectional: it clears as recorded posterity, and the car stops at grade one.
The sinkhole
Decades of erosion were blank relative to every state before the collapse. The verdict of "had the light not been red" changes with the index, not with the time.
Bizet and Verdi
The residue splits between both-French and both-Italian: both woulds false, both mights true, the grades strictly between zero and one, with no ordering to adjudicate.
Antecedent strengthening, transitivity and contraposition are not secured by anything; a strengthened antecedent is a different perturbation, and the witnesses show the failures.
