Moments

Before Alpha, 4,600 to 4,500 million years ago

The Earth coalesces from the raw material at the birth of the Solar System. It goes from a cloud of dust, growing more and more with each new particle, until it has become its full, planetary sized bulk of Essence.

From Alpha to Beta, 4,500 to 4,100 million years ago

The Earth is recovering from its giant impact, now orbited by the baby form our moon. The Hadean wraps up and it appears as though the Solar System is settling down, upon the surface of this dried up rock.

From Beta to Gamma, 4,100 to 3,800 million years ago

The Late Heavy Bombardment booms on in full force. Holes are blown into the surfaces of the planets as the Hadean deals its unexpected second blow. But all the while water accumulates...
