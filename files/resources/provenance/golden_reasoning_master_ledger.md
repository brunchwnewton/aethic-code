### The lesson v58 records

**Reference checks cannot see structure errors.** An empty subsection heading dangles nothing, duplicates nothing, and leaves every label resolving — so every verification run at delivery passed. This is the *second* instance of the same class: the α-block loss (v40) was invisible for the same reason, since a missing block also dangles nothing.

Both were found by reading rather than grepping. The ledger records the practice that follows: **periodic reading of spans, not only periodic checking of references** — and, specifically, that any insertion made *before* an anchor must be checked against what that anchor is the first content *of*.
