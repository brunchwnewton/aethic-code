\begin{abstract}
\noindent This paper presents the framework of Aethic reasoning and organizes the dependencies among its semantic, algebraic, and physical constructions. Its central move is that determinacy is a two-place relation between an attribute and an observer's information state, the openness it relativizes being tense-free. What a state leaves open is open rather than unknown, so a uniform, noncontextual, measurement-independent relabeling of openness as ignorance forces a single sample space that the Bell statistics exclude. Whether alternatives exclude one another is content a state carries, which splits superposition into an exclusive and an agreeing regime. A state is admissible only if some proper descendant of it is safe, which is the collapse law and, over a space of paths under a complete and persistent record structure, fixes the quantum--classical boundary as the record partition; collapse is a change of index rather than an event. Pruning ranges over admissible, non-negligible components at every refinement depth. One posited weighting axiom, the path kernel on a fixed finite domain, adds the quantitative face: a Hilbert representation of the admissible clusters, record observables as self-adjoint operators, the squared-norm probability assignment, and the projection postulate as the representation of a stipulated record-compatible restriction. Two representative consequences: the two-tag interferometer exhibits both composition regimes, and an unread which-path record and an unread friend's laboratory fall under one admissibility criterion. The main assumptions are the posited weights and the principle bridging formal invalidity to physical unrealizability; the open physical question is whether a laboratory's records can be kept from escaping, on which the framework's divergence from universal unitarity turns.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Every realist reading of quantum mechanics inherits one debt: saying what unperformed measurements would have shown. Bell and Kochen--Specker close the obvious route to paying it---a standing valuation of the unmeasured---and the dominant Lewis--Stalnaker possible-worlds tradition and standard structural-causal semantics evaluate at complete world or model states, a valuation wearing modal dress. The debt is therefore specific: counterfactual \emph{substance} without counterfactual \emph{definiteness}---truth-apt \emph{woulds} and \emph{mights} with no valuation underneath. That is why a paper about the measurement problem begins with the semantics of counterfactuals, and why the semantics it needs cannot be borrowed.

\textit{The move.} The framework's one move answers the debt: \emph{determinacy is a two-place relation between an attribute and an information state}, never a one-place property of spacetime-located facts, and the openness it relativizes is tense-free. The open state is the \emph{blank}, the framework's central modal posit---structural, occupying the position where classical bookkeeping holds a hidden value, not a gloss on ignorance. And the move's reach is deliberately audacious, because the framework refuses to run two bookkeepings for one primitive: a road collapses into a sinkhole after decades of unobserved erosion,
(a \emph{disagreeing} case in the vocabulary of \S\ref{sec:union}, the eroded ground being itself a record---chosen as the move's reach, not its evidence),
and relative to every observer on that road the erosion was open---resolving at the moment each learned, never at a moment the ground moved, the unlearned past exactly as open as the unformed future, the points unchanged and only the index moving. The case returns in full, guarded against the tensed paraphrase that would tame it, at \S\ref{sec:checks}.

\textit{Terms.} The framework's vocabulary is small and recurs throughout, so it is fixed here rather than assembled from context. An \emph{Aethus} is an information state: what an observer's records say, with everything they do not say left open. A \emph{blank} is one of those open attributes---open in the constructive sense, not merely unrecorded. Two alternatives stand in \emph{disagreeing superposition} when each states the other's negation, and in \emph{agreeing superposition} when neither does; the difference is the paper's central formal distinction, and it is what ordinary usage of ``or'' runs together. Agreement is not coherence: agreeing and disagreeing superposition form a qualitative category of Aethic composition, defined before weights or dynamics exist, while coherence and decoherence belong to the quantitative physical realization of that structure, and neither word is a synonym of the other. \emph{Aethic reasoning} is reasoning whose objects are Aethae and whose transformations---refinement, reopening, pruning, weighting---preserve their indexed partiality rather than completing them into total histories or worlds. \emph{Validity} is the property an information state has when some proper descendant of it is safe, none of its own proper descendants forced into contradiction; \emph{pruning} is the removal of the states that lack it, and the \emph{residue} is what pruning leaves. A \emph{child} of an \Aethus{} is a multiplicative extension of it by joint assertion. A \emph{proper child} is a structurally visible component of an admissible declared splitting, a cell of a carving the \Aethus{} holds whose coefficient is nonzero and not measure-zero relative to the rest of the splitting, and a \emph{proper descendant} is a proper child at any depth; that comparison is \emph{relative negligibility}, and at ordinary real weights it means only that the coefficient is positive. Proper childhood is transitive under the algebra companion's admissibility and coefficient-composition assumptions, so it reaches every depth, and the third postulate ranges over it, which is why a valid component carried at negligible weight stays represented but supplies no floor for validity. The algebraic companion owns these relations and states them precisely \cite{AethusTree}; later sections refer back here.

\textit{What this overview is.} This is a companion overview rather than an independent research paper. It states the framework's proposal, follows one dependency chain through the program, and gives each principal result with its essential assumptions, its owner paper and its status (Table~\ref{tab:results}); it claims no result its owner papers do not establish. The chain runs: determinacy is indexed, and blankness is tense-free and constructive (\S\ref{sec:books}, \S\ref{sec:nogo}); composition distinguishes exclusion from openness (\S\ref{sec:union}); the third postulate constrains admissible proper descendants (\S\S\ref{sec:postulates}--\ref{sec:fixedpoint}); a quantitative axiom supplies the finite physical representation (\S\ref{sec:nexus}); and the applications show the framework's reach and its limits (\S\S\ref{sec:powerset}--\ref{sec:two_tag_brief}). The counterfactual semantics of \S\ref{sec:semantics} is the chain's first worked example, the interferometer of \S\ref{sec:two_tag_brief} its second, and the detector--friend comparison its third.

\section{Counterfactuals Without Possible Worlds}\label{sec:semantics}

\subsection{The Book-Keeping Picture}\label{sec:books}

Begin with an observer at a moment, and collect everything their situation settles: every attribute whose answer their information fixes, directly or by deduction. That totality is their \Aethus. It is not a mental state and not a credence function; it is the complete set of facts-for-them, closed under deduction, with every attribute it does not settle left blank. Fixed as objects: an attribute $q$ is a question with an answer space; an \Aethus{} $A$ is a deductively closed assignment of answers to some of the attributes, writing $A(q)=v$ when $q$ is \emph{stated} in $A$ with value $v$, and $A(q)=\ast$ when $q$ is \emph{blank} (the notation is written for elementary attributes, where unstated and blank coincide; the composite case is separated two paragraphs below). Deductive closure means the books contain their own consequences: if the stated entries of $A$ jointly entail $q=v$, then $A(q)=v$. The block universe is one, and it is rendered relative to this \Aethus: one rendering per information state, the observer's history of learning forming a chain of Aethae whose renderings refine one another, never a plurality of spacetimes. A blank attribute's alternatives are carried \emph{on the books} as open entries of that block's description. Where the alternatives themselves reside---whether at other possible worlds, as the classical tradition houses them, or somewhere far stranger---is a question this section deliberately leaves unsettled: for now, everything that follows can be read as pure bookkeeping, and the reader who suspects it is \emph{only} bookkeeping is encouraged to hold that suspicion carefully, because section~\ref{sec:nogo} is addressed to them by name.

Two consequences of the book-keeping picture do early work. First, blankness is \emph{tense-free}: an attribute is blank or stated relative to an \Aethus, and where the event it concerns sits in proper time is a separate matter entirely. The observer's unlearned past is exactly as open, on this accounting, as their unformed future---and this shift of blankness's home, from the future alone to anywhere in proper time the index has not reached, is the paper's recurring axis: it powers the sinkhole below, the delayed-choice dissolution of section~\ref{sec:powerset}, and the strangeness of the block universe that the collection will price. Where classical metaphysics lets time carry openness, this framework makes openness a fact about the index and lets time carry nothing but geometry. Second, blanks are \emph{constructive}: a blank attribute is not a gap awaiting a hidden value but a complete informational entry in its own right, encoding the state of the world relative to that \Aethus{} without any determinate value underneath. And blankness is a \emph{retrieval} fact, not a bookkeeping absence: $q$ is blank relative to $A$ when the books leave $q$'s \emph{entire} answer space open, so that $q$ retrieves as all of its states at once. For an elementary attribute---one question, one answer space---that is the same thing as $A(q)=\ast$. For a composite attribute---a conjunction, which deductive closure makes present whenever its parts are and which is an attribute of the space in its own right---the two come apart, and the reason is its answer space: a composite's answer space is the \emph{product} of its parts', so $X \wedge y$ ranges over four states, not two. With $X$ blank and $y$ stated true, two of the four are open; the composite is unstated, $A(q)=\ast$, yet not blank but \emph{mixed}, and the postulate of \S\ref{sec:postulates} speaks only to the blank grade. (The companion's names: \emph{directly} blank for the mere absence of the composite from the books, \emph{conceptually} blank for the retrieval sense, the mixed status the gap between them. Its dichotomy theorem splits a mixed attribute into stated and blank parts so that the postulate can be applied to each \emph{part}; it confers no status on the whole, and it is the whole that a reopening must return to blank, \S\ref{sec:perturbation}.) What would rescue a mixed composite is a \emph{coarsening}: read the conjunction as two-valued, true-or-false, and with $X$ blank it is open over both, hence ``blank.'' That is a presentation of the attribute, not the attribute; the companion's \emph{Decomposition Neutrality}, the principle that a stated-attribute has no privileged decomposition, fixes the attribute's answer space as its parts' product, and the dual-peg reading of the antecedent that this paper adopts is a substantive interpretive choice beside it, not a consequence of it, a rival reading being left open rather than excluded. The lean is booked at \S\ref{sec:close}. The classical habit of hearing ``unknown'' as ``determinate but unrevealed'' is precisely the habit this paper exists to break, and section~\ref{sec:nogo} will show where the break is forced --- and by what: empirically in the agreeing sector, architecturally everywhere else.

\subsection{Evaluation by Perturbation}\label{sec:perturbation}

The counterfactual semantics is now three steps, and none of them is travel.

To evaluate \emph{if X had happened, Y would have happened} from an \Aethus{} $A$: first, \emph{reopen} the antecedent attribute---return the page of $A$'s books on which X's attribute is stated to blank, together with everything that rests on it at the chosen scope, and nothing else; second, \emph{restate} the attribute to the antecedent's value; third, \emph{re-prune}---let the framework's consistency constraints (part two's postulates, stated in section~\ref{sec:postulates}) eliminate the alternatives that can no longer stand---and \emph{read the residue}. Each step is an operation with a definition. Write $A \ominus q$ for the \emph{reopening} of $q$ in $A$: the maximal deductively closed restriction of $A$ in which $q$ is blank at scope $\sigma$, written $A \ominus_{\sigma} q$: what ``rests on'' $q$ is read at the setting, $\sigma$ ranging over three named settings---\emph{evidential-minimal}, \emph{unidirectional} (this paper's default, $\sigma = \mathrm{uni}$, suppressed in the notation where unambiguous), and \emph{bidirectional}---fixed under the second fine point below. Maximality is taken under the declared priorities (laws over particulars, an entry's generality read off its own quantificational form), a policy the owner paper states and whose presentation-invariant derivation remains open. Where those underdetermine---two \emph{particulars} jointly entailing the reopened entry tie, and quantificational form is silent between them---the reopening is \emph{multi-valued}, and the tie enters the residue as co-open pockets feeding the ordinary might/would/grade machinery, as Bizet--Verdi does below. Under-determination is residue structure, not defect. Write $\mathcal{R}(\cdot)$ for the \emph{reduced form}: the set of maximal consistent refinements surviving the pruning, each refinement a pocket of co-open content, the whole carrying a weight measure $w$. The refinements settle each blank to one of its values: the blanks of the semantics are scenario blanks, each attribute's single-peg carving belonging to every evaluation state's standing structure, so that a blank is the union of the attribute's determinate states rather than a bare filling of open values \cite{AethusSemantics}. The \emph{perturbation} by an antecedent $X$ (stating attribute $q_X$ to value $x$) is then
\begin{equation*}
\pi^{\sigma}_X(A) \;=\; \mathcal{R}\big(\ (A \ominus_{\sigma} q_X) + \{q_X {=}\, x\} \,\big),
\end{equation*}
and the modal operators receive their truth conditions directly on it:
\begin{align*}
A \models X \mathrel{\Box\!\!\rightarrow} Y \quad &\text{iff} \quad \text{every pocket of } \pi_X(A) \text{ settles } Y \text{ affirmatively}, \\
A \models X \mathrel{\Diamond\!\!\rightarrow} Y \quad &\text{iff} \quad \text{some pocket of } \pi_X(A) \text{ does}, \\
\mathrm{P}_A(Y \,\Vert\, X) \;&=\; \frac{w\big(\pi_X(A) \restriction Y\big)}{w\big(\pi_X(A)\big)},
\end{align*}
where $\pi_X(A) \restriction Y$ collects the $Y$-affirming pockets. \emph{Would}, \emph{might}, and \emph{the probability that} are thereby one operator read at three grains---universal over the residue, existential over it, and its weighted proportion---and where the residue splits, the framework does not force a binary verdict but reports the graded fact.

Three fine points fix the operator's behavior.

\textit{Two operations: indicative selection and subjunctive reopening.} First, the perturbation is two operations selected by mood, not one rule read at two limits: under the \emph{indicative} setting the evaluation is selection over the standing residue, the pockets in which the antecedent holds and nothing else; under the \emph{subjunctive} settings it is computed from restrictions of the state itself, the priority-selected maximal closed blank restrictions, one or several as the priorities select or tie, each restated and re-pruned with the residue the union over them, and never by completing the state and reopening its pockets one by one, which computes a different operation (the companion's three-attribute example shows the two can differ in verdict). The two moods share the pruning and the weighting and are otherwise distinct operations; no coincidence condition between them is claimed, and the tradition's ``two modes'' are these two operations, not limits of one rule. For the \emph{agnostic} Adams evaluator---death stated, shooter blank at weight $w_O$ on Oswald---the subjunctive reopens the shooter in the state itself at posterity scope, the recorded posterity of the shot clearing with it, the death included, in every pocket at once; with no conspiracy on the books nothing regenerates the death, so the would comes out \emph{false}, the might holds through second-shooter pockets carried at small positive weight $w_S$, visible against the rest, and the grade is small and positive, $w_S$ against the whole residue, for every $w_O$; pockets at exactly zero weight would be no proper children and would sustain neither reading. The interpolating grade $1 - w_O$ that a pocket-wise procedure produced belonged to that procedure and is withdrawn with it; what an evaluator all but certain it wasn't Oswald means is carried by the indicative, which returns true at grade one. Two commitments of the operation follow and are owned: centering fails for stated antecedents, since exempting a stated antecedent from reopening would privilege the actual outcome over its alternative, the toss that landed heads over the landing tails, and no postulate supplies that privilege, the alternatives being symmetric; and credence enters through the indicative alone, the subjunctive's grade tracking only the standing weight of what the reopened state leaves. A mood-sensitive graded verdict for uncertain evaluators is not something a binary semantics produces. (The previously run checks---streetlight, Morgenbesser, Bizet--Verdi, backtracking---all have uniform pockets, so their verdicts are unchanged; and under the indicative setting the operator computes conditional probability \emph{outright}, since selection followed by renormalization is conditioning by the theorem of section~\ref{sec:coupling}. Adams's thesis---that indicative credence goes by conditional credence---thereby arrives as an \emph{identity of the indicative setting} rather than a constraint imposed on it \cite{adams1975logic} --- placement, per the column discipline of section~\ref{sec:coupling}.) Lewis's ``small miracle''---the minimal law-violation that manufactures a foreign world---becomes an auditable bookkeeping operation: no world is manufactured; a page of the books is reopened at a named place.

\textit{The scope convention.} Second, the reopening's \emph{scope} is the semantics' one declared conventional parameter, and it is structured: one parameter, three named settings, selected by mood. \emph{Evidential-minimal} clears the antecedent's entry and its deductive cone only, keeping whatever enjoys independent support. (The indicative is none of these settings: it selects within the evaluator's own \Aethus{} rather than reopening it.) \emph{Unidirectional} clears the antecedent together with the generics wired to it on the direction convention's forward side (its recorded effects; the direction an emergent, perspective-relative reading mined from the attributes' disjointness content \cite{Aethus})---the standard subjunctive's setting and the default. \emph{Bidirectional} runs the clearing across every wired generic, direction-blind, both sides at once---the backtracking setting (section~\ref{sec:battery}). Mood selects the operation, and for the subjunctive the setting; the reopening acts on the state itself at that scope, never pocket by pocket. Two operations; scope is the subjunctive's one declared conventional parameter, stated, visible, and movable rather than hidden inside a metric, and booked on the closing ledger as a convention, since it does verdict-determining work; one further clause was booked beside them in earlier prints and is now recorded as a reading rather than a convention: conjunctive antecedents reopen \emph{conjunct-wise}---each conjunct's attribute returned to blank, its ground cleared---which, under the dual-peg reading of conjunction, a substantive choice of antecedent interpretation, is a consequence of the three steps as defined, once blankness is read at the grade section~\ref{sec:books} fixes and the antecedent is read by the \emph{dual-peg reading}, each conjunct's peg a separate argument of the reopening. That reading is the framework's reading of ``and'' in an antecedent; the two-valued attribute \emph{is $X \wedge y$ true?} is a coarsening of the composite, a different attribute, and on it nothing is cleared. The first step reopens \emph{the antecedent attribute}---returns it to blank, in the retrieval sense: its whole answer space open, its carving kept, since the carving states no value and the maximal restriction retains it, so that the reopened attribute is a scenario blank whose pockets are its values---and only then does the second step restate; the composite conjunction is that attribute, since the operator takes the antecedent as its argument and closure makes the conjunction an attribute of the space, and its status is read of it rather than delegated to a part (section~\ref{sec:books}'s one lean on the companion's Decomposition Neutrality, which the companion derives at length \cite{Aethus}). So, on the dual-peg reading, a conjunctive target is returned to blank only with every conjunct blank; a rival reading of the antecedent is not excluded by Decomposition Neutrality, which fixes the attribute's decomposition and not the antecedent's interpretation. Reading the conjunction as one attribute and blanking it \emph{maximally}---keeping an already-actual conjunct and its ground because blanking the other already unstates the conjunction---leaves the target directly blank but \emph{mixed}, one part present, which is not superposition at all; it is rescued only by calling the target open insofar as its blank component is, and that delegation of the whole's status to one part is what section~\ref{sec:books}'s reading of a composite excludes; the Cautious Monotony result of section~\ref{sec:deletion} runs on the derived reading---stated here, graded there.\footnote{The Adams pair falls out of the scope menu, and each sentence is best run from the evaluator who would naturally assert it---a small display of the indexing doing pragmatic work. The \emph{agnostic's} indicative---\emph{if Oswald didn't shoot him, someone else did}---runs evidential-minimal by selection: the death, independently supported, stays stated, the surviving pockets are the someone-else ones, and the indicative comes out true (the \emph{believer's} indicative, having no pocket in which the antecedent holds, has an empty selection and no indicative verdict, the counterfactual there being the subjunctive's). The \emph{believer's} subjunctive---\emph{if Oswald hadn't shot him, someone else would have}---runs unidirectional by reopening: the death, downstream of the shot, is cleared, and absent a stated conspiracy the would fails while the mights survive. Morgenbesser's coin still passes for free on the subjunctive setting (the coin is not in the bet's causal posterity, so it stays fixed). Two operations throughout, mood selecting between them.} Third, the inventory: the semantics adds exactly \emph{one} new modal primitive, the perturbation operator itself; validity, weight, and the pruning are all inherited from machinery the framework needs for independent reasons. Rounding that one down to zero would be a refutable boast, and the theory is stronger for admitting it.

\textit{The dependency structure, booked.} And one further piece of equipment must be booked while we are counting: the reopening is well-defined only because the books carry a \emph{dependency structure}---which entries rest on which---so that $A \ominus q$ has a determinate answer (set-valued where the declared priorities tie or rank incomparable maxima). For a bare deductively closed theory it would have none at all: $\{p,\ p \to q\}$ can lose $q$ by dropping either member, the remainder-set problem the belief-revision literature was built around \cite{alchourron1985logic}. That dependency structure is extra structure, it is exactly where an ordering-theorist will look for the deleted ordering's ghost, and it therefore goes on the ledger.

Three features distinguish this from the possible-worlds treatment at the level of machinery, before any philosophy is spent. The evaluation never leaves the evaluator's own structure: the perturbation is an operation on the books, not a search across worlds.\footnote{Every documented language runs modality through one shared determinate ledger, as though each speaker were attached to a single complete fact-state with possibility bolted on as an operator over it; Aethic reasoning reverses the attachment at the person---you are attached to your \emph{partial} information state, blanks and all, and the first-person pronoun points at the \Aethus{}, not through it. The cost of that reversal is priced as the third cost of \S\ref{sec:close}; the companion assembles the typological and developmental evidence \cite{Aethus}.} The evaluation is \emph{checkable}: each alleged dependence of Y on X is tested by a single perturbation, with the verdict read off the residue, so causal claims become audit-shaped rather than similarity-shaped. And the evaluation is \emph{indexed}: the same counterfactual can receive different verdicts from different Aethae without contradiction, because the verdicts are facts about differently-informed books, not competing claims about one ledger.

\subsection{The First Headline: The Ordering Is Deleted, Not Refined}\label{sec:deletion}

The Lewis--Stalnaker tradition \cite{lewis1973counterfactuals, stalnaker1968theory} evaluates the same sentence by locating the closest possible worlds where X holds and asking whether Y holds there, with \emph{closest} supplied by a similarity ordering over worlds. The comparison is sharpest at the level of the clauses themselves. Lewis's official truth condition, in its standard form:
\begin{equation*}
w \models X \mathrel{\Box\!\!\rightarrow} Y \quad \text{iff} \quad \text{some } (X \wedge Y)\text{-world is closer to } w \text{, by } \leq_w \text{, than any } (X \wedge \neg Y)\text{-world},
\end{equation*}
(vacuous truth aside), where $\leq_w$ is the similarity ordering centered on $w$. Set it against the clause of section~\ref{sec:perturbation} and perform the visual audit: the evaluation point changed type, from a world $w$ to an \Aethus{} $A$; the search across worlds became an operation, $\pi_X$, on the evaluation point's own structure; and \emph{the parameter $\leq_w$ appears nowhere on the right-hand side}. Fifty years of refinement adjusted that parameter's weights---laws over particular facts, perfect match over approximate---without dislodging the structural complaint that the ordering is imposed rather than derived: nothing in the picture says why similarity, or which similarity, tracks the truth of counterfactuals. A reader from the belief-revision literature will recognize mined ground here, and it is walked knowingly: Lewis himself proved that premise semantics---evaluation by consistent adjustments to a premise set, no ordering in sight---is equivalent to ordering semantics \cite{lewis1981ordering}, Grove proved the analogous representation theorem for belief revision \cite{grove1988two}, and the perturbation is, formally, contraction-then-expansion in the AGM mold \cite{alchourron1985logic}. Non-appearance of the parameter is therefore not the standard; non-recoverability is. The paper's considered claim is calibrated accordingly: the ordering's work is \emph{derived from and localized in} the deductive dependency structure of the books, rather than free-floating over worlds---and the recoverability contest now stands split, one stage closed and one posed. Closed: the would-clause's consequence relation \emph{violates Cautious Monotony}, by the countermodel displayed at the end of this paragraph (its natural-language face an ordinary explaining-away pattern, and the companion separates the cases cleanly \cite{Aethus}: the bare conjunction \emph{if it had rained and the lawn had been wet} should leave hearers divided (a prediction, not yet a report: no elicited-judgment study of this case exists)---many keeping the sprinkler run that actually wetted it, so that the gutters still overflow---while the \emph{anyway}-variant shows the reopened parse is grammaticalized in ordinary usage, a marker whose whole job is to reopen a conjunct's ground; the countermodel runs on the \emph{uniform reading}, under which a conjunctive antecedent is reopened whole, each conjunct's ground cleared even where a per-conjunct application of the pocket-wise selection rule would pass an already-holding conjunct through --- given the dual-peg interpretation of conjunctive antecedents, reopening must blank each constituent attribute, which section~\ref{sec:perturbation} derives from that interpretation rather than stipulates --- so the headline is graded: \emph{closed, on the dual-peg reading of conjunctive antecedents}---a substantive interpretive choice that Decomposition Neutrality does not itself supply, as the companion now states (section~\ref{sec:perturbation}, with the derivation's proofs in the companion \cite{Aethus})---with whether the bare natural-language conjunction defaults to that reading a question about the language---exactly what the instrument already in the literature \cite{ciardelli2018} adapts directly to adjudicate---and not about the operator); since preferential relations satisfy Cautious Monotony, and ranked relations---those a similarity ordering represents---are preferential, the relation agrees with no smooth preferential relation on all of its evaluable verdicts, so it is not preferential and \emph{a fortiori} not ordering-representable at the consequence-relation level---the Lewis and Grove equivalences notwithstanding, proved as they were for single-output revision on unindexed, binary-verdict theories (the Kraus--Lehmann--Magidor audit, the countermodel, and the surviving core---Reflexivity, Right Weakening, And, Cut wherever the reopenings are nested, Left Logical Equivalence at attribute grade, Or---are the companion's \cite{Aethus}).

\noindent\textit{The countermodel, in outline.} Four attributes suffice---rain, the sprinkler, the lawn's wetness with the sprinkler as its ground, and overflowing gutters---and the failure sits at one place: a conjunctive antecedent reopens a conjunct's ground and so resurrects pockets the plain antecedent pruned. The clearing is not an exception to downstream-only clearing. Reopening both conjuncts \emph{and} clearing their supports is derived, on the dual-peg reading, as the only reading that returns the composite to blank, since under closure a support left stated would simply re-derive its conjunct; what remains empirical is only whether the English conjunction tracks this operator. The books, the laws, the derivation, and the residues are the companions' \cite{Aethus, AethusSemantics}.

The witness is stated on the dual-peg reading of a conjunctive antecedent, on which a conjunctive target is returned to blank only with every conjunct blank; that reading is a substantive commitment of the semantics, and a rival reading of the antecedent, blanking the conjunction as one attribute and keeping an already-actual conjunct, is not excluded by Decomposition Neutrality, which fixes the attribute's decomposition and not the antecedent's interpretation. On the rival reading the witness does not arise. The non-preferentiality result is therefore scoped to the uniform dual-peg reading, and the semantics paper carries the comparison of readings and the graded-representation question it leaves open \cite{AethusSemantics}.



Two further exhibits complete the deletion's formal face, at one sentence each. \emph{Completion-blindness}: two states identical in every stated attribute and every weight apart from the cross attribute over a blank's alternatives, declared false in one and left undeclared in the other, possess identical completion classes --- the completion map is a projection that discards exactly that information, every completion resolving the blank and destroying the co-presence the entry describes --- while splitting the verdict of a conditional keyed to that attribute; no function of a state's total completions computes the verdicts, supervaluation included, so the classical vocabulary is not re-parameterized but expressively short. And \emph{imaging}: the tradition's own probabilistic repair --- Lewis's 1976 proposal, moving each world's probability mass to its closest antecedent-world --- consumes the similarity ordering pointwise, the transport plan being the ordering itself, while the graded verdict here routes the mass through the blank intermediate, consulting dependency and validity only; the deletion extends to the probabilistic tradition's own fix.

The Aethic replacement should not be described as a better ordering. No world-similarity ordering is primitive. Its work is split among structures that were independently needed: the \emph{dependency structure} of the books, which fixes what a reopening holds; \emph{validity}---the binary fact of whether an alternative survives the re-pruning at all---and \emph{weight}---the graded structure on what survives. Nothing ranks the surviving alternatives against an ideal; they survive or they do not, and among the survivors weight measures rather than ranks. This is the paper's first headline because it is a deletion with an address: the parameter philosophers spent five decades tuning is identified as an artifact of housing alternatives elsewhere, and it vanishes the moment the alternatives come home.

The deletion lands twice: in the formal semantics of natural-language modality, where Kratzer's ordering source is the parameter removed, and in the belief-revision tradition, where the Ramsey test's addition against revision is the indicative--subjunctive seam seen from its own side \cite{kratzer2012modals, gardenfors1988knowledge}; both are developed in the semantics paper.



\subsection{The Standard Battery}\label{sec:battery}

The adversarial cases the tradition built fall to the two operations without additional machinery, and the semantics paper works each \cite{AethusSemantics}: Bizet--Verdi comes out as a might-tie with the would failing, since the reopened residue holds both nationalities; Morgenbesser's coin passes on the subjunctive setting because the coin lies outside the bet's causal posterity and stays fixed; the Adams pair divides between the operations, the indicative selecting and the subjunctive reopening, which is where credence enters through the indicative alone; backtracking is the scope parameter's posterity setting; and the continuous case, which a whole-world similarity ordering cannot grade, is graded by the standing weights of the pockets the perturbation leaves. None of these is an axiom of the semantics; each is the operation run on the case.

\subsection{Worked Checks, Compressed}\label{sec:checks}

Two examples carry the semantics' weight in the long paper, and they compress well.

\emph{The streetlight.} An observer drives through an intersection; the light was green; had it been red, the driver would have stopped. On the unidirectional setting the reopening clears ``proceeded'' as recorded posterity of the light, restates red, and re-prunes to the alternatives in which the standing disposition meets red and the car stops: would-stopped at grade one, the flanking facts untouched. On the evidential-minimal setting the proceeding entry survives the narrower clearing and closure then excludes red, so no admissible intermediate exists and the run is infeasible rather than vacuous, which is the scope parameter doing its work.



\emph{The sinkhole.} A driver is stopped at a red light; two minutes later a sinkhole opens just ahead, where the car would have been on a green, the erosion behind it having run underground for decades, unobserved. Relative to the driver's later state, which states the sinkhole and its history, \emph{had the light been green, the car would have met the hole} holds; relative to the state at the light, where the sinkhole and its history are blank, it does not, since that state's continuations include ones in which the car crosses the same ground with no hole beneath it. The collapse produced a record, but a record's existence resolves nothing for an observer until that observer's successor state comes to state it; and the later verdict does not replace the earlier one, which remains true relative to the earlier state, the erosion's temporal precedence supplying no determination at that index. The unlearned past is as open as the unformed future. That is tense-free indexing in one case. The same indexing blocks the recursive construction of one determinate history: dependencies established at different indices compose only once a common admissible index is established, both survive transport to it, and a composition rule is given, so what is blocked is the unlicensed construction and not a common mechanism history where those conditions hold. Both are run in full in the causal companion \cite{AethusCausality}.



\section{A Note Against Deflation}\label{sec:nogo}

Before anything further is built, one objection must be met, because everything depends on how it is met---and the meeting is a pricing, not a refutation: empirical where records run out, architectural everywhere else. The objection is aimed at everything granted so far, sections~\ref{sec:books} through~\ref{sec:deletion} taken together, and the note is accordingly a \emph{conditional} result---granted that ledger, deflation fails---which is the form that makes it auditable. The objection says: all of the above is classical metaphysics under new labels. Call attributes ``blank'' if you like; a blank is just an unknown; an unknown has a determinate value behind it; your ``superpositions'' are ignorance with a vocabulary. If this deflationary reading were available, part one would be a notational variant of the possible-worlds picture it claims to replace, and part two would be sleight of hand.

The reading is available only at a price, and the argument has one fork. The target is exact: a deflation that keeps what a state leaves open as constructive openness and adds a determinate value at the same index contradicts the state's own content, which is the internal preservation constraint; a deflation that instead translates openness into ignorance uniformly, noncontextually, and with measurement independence posits a lossless assignment whose image is a single sample space, and that image is what the Bell statistics exclude, under the commitments D1--D5 stated in the determinacy paper \cite{AethusRealist}. Neither horn uniquely selects information-state indexing: a contextual assignment, and a deflation confined to the classical sector where the statistics never bite, lie outside the targeted class and are not eliminated by the obstruction. What the framework claims against them is architectural, that one semantics applied uniformly across the classical and quantum sectors is cheaper than two bookkeepings with a cut between them, and that claim is a cost comparison the reader is free to dispute, not a theorem.



The closure earns the headline the introduction opened on: the framework provides \emph{counterfactual substance without counterfactual definiteness}. Counterfactual discourse remains fully truth-apt---\emph{would}, \emph{might}, and \emph{the probability that} receive verdicts by the semantics of section~\ref{sec:perturbation}---while the assumption that all counterfactual measurement outcomes possess simultaneous determinate values, the assumption Bell-type theorems quantify over, fails \emph{natively}: an agreeing superposition admits no alternative in which its attribute takes a single value, so the joint valuation the theorems require does not exist to be forbidden. This is precisely the combination the quantum realist program has needed: enough counterfactual structure to say what experiments would have shown, and no hidden valuation for the theorems to kill. Hidden-variable programs supply the first half of the demand at the cost of the second (they say what would have shown, by carrying a valuation of the kind the theorems restrict, noncontextual and measurement-independent; a contextual, nonlocal program such as Bohmian mechanics survives them, as the framework acknowledges); instrumentalisms supply neither; the Aethic structure supplies both. The collection begins immediately, with the structural move that the note has just made safe to state at full ontological strength.

One question is answered here rather than left to fester: a classical filtration also retains its past $\sigma$-algebras, so the framework's retention of indices is not what distinguishes it from classical epistemology; what distinguishes it is that yesterday's openness is present content at yesterday's index, consulted by the counterfactual clauses, rather than a fact about yesterday reported from today.



\section{Physical Disjunction, and the Union Move}\label{sec:union} The semantics so far treats one blank attribute at a time, and the note above has just argued what such an attribute \emph{is}: open, co-present alternative content, permanently indexed. But that argument also creates a debt, and it must be paid immediately: if the alternatives are co-present---physically here, in the one block---why does any observer ever see just one? A framework that quietly borrowed mutual exclusivity back from classical metaphysics at this point would be undoing its own closure. The union move is the payment, and it is therefore not an optional stylistic commitment but the forced next step: now that co-presence is earned rather than posited, the account of non-interaction must be rebuilt from materials the framework actually owns---and it is the move that will carry the physics. Its one-sentence form, the framework's deepest inversion: exclusivity is an \emph{achievement}, not a default --- stated interior content that some configurations of alternatives carry and others simply lack.\footnote{Two lineages, since this move is squarely a revision in the neighborhood of disjunction. Against quantum logic \cite{birkhoff1936} and the empiricism about logic it prompted \cite{putnam1968}: the revision there is to the \emph{consequence relation} --- a non-distributive lattice of propositions, connectives reinterpreted, deduction weakened --- while here deduction stays classical and the propositional lattice distributive, and what changes is the \emph{primitive stock}: physical union enters as a new composition primitive --- the jointness of \emph{or}, per the display's note below --- rather than as a reinterpretation of the connectives. So Putnam's question does land on logic, and receives a changed answer: the empirical matter is not the consequence relation but the primitive inventory, and, downstream of it, which exclusions the world's records state --- with the one distributivity question the framework does own priced as an explicit hypothesis of the state algebra (the unweighted semiring of the algebra companion), not of the logic \cite{Aethus}.
At the grade a quantum logician will insist on, the differentia is \emph{where} the revision lives---in the state algebra rather than in the consequence relation---and not whether there is one; the framework claims the location, and prices the algebra's distributivity accordingly: the algebra companion exhibits its failure outside restricted fragments, so it holds as a hypothesis on those fragments only \cite{AethusTree}.}
Classical metaphysics treats mutual exclusivity as primitive: alternatives, whatever they are, exclude one another by nature, and \emph{or} in its physical sense simply inherits that exclusion. The Aethic proposal inverts the inheritance. Enumerating a blank attribute's alternatives---the disjunction---is one job; \emph{mutual exclusion} between the enumerated alternatives is a separate job; and the framework hands the second job to the \emph{interiors} of the enumerated pockets, where it is done, when it is done, by stated content --- and the content in question is a \emph{logical declaration}, not a qualitative one. Where the interiors state the mutual negations --- each alternative's cross content with its rivals declared \emph{false}, side by side --- the alternatives exclude: co-present but mutually barred, and this is the \emph{disagreeing} superposition, the kind every example so far has involved, and the kind whose macroscopic ubiquity makes classical metaphysics feel true. But where \emph{nothing is said} --- the interiors silent, no negation declared, no affirmation either --- the joint standing of the alternatives remains open, and this is the \emph{agreeing} superposition. (The familiar gloss that agreeing alternatives ``interact'' is pedagogy, and it is retired here before it can mislead: the dichotomy is logic --- declared-false against undeclared --- and which class of physical interaction actually runs on an agreeing pair is fixed by the mechanics that consume the logic, not by the logic itself; in particular, quantum interference is not mechanical interaction wearing a mask.) As structure: with $e_{ij}$ the cross attribute carrying the joint standing of alternatives $i$ and $j$,
\begin{equation*}
\text{disagreeing:}\;\; \{a_1, \dots, a_n \,;\, e_{ij} \text{ declared false}\} \qquad\qquad \text{agreeing:}\;\; \{a_1, \dots, a_n \,;\, e_{ij} \text{ undeclared}\},
\end{equation*}
one attribute's worth of difference, carrying the entire classical--quantum divide.\footnote{The notation invites a classical misreading, and the concept beneath it is perhaps the framework's most alien. The two regimes are not two configurations of one classical substance --- a list of alternatives with an interaction flag toggled on or off. The union principle beneath this display holds that composition itself comes in two logical substances: alongside classical conjunction there is a \emph{primitive union} --- the \emph{jointness} of \emph{or} --- under which logical disjunction realizes physically as joint occupation of the block, co-presence with \emph{openness} as its native character --- a jointness usable but, like \emph{and} itself, not further describable, it being a logical primitive. That substance is the \emph{default} composition of co-present alternatives; classical mutual exclusivity is not primitive but manufactured, the nested achievement in which interior negations forbid interaction while permitting simultaneous presence. The familiar physical \emph{or} is thereby, in the fuller taxonomy, entirely a subcategory of the disagreeing case --- and the agreeing case is categorically alien to classical metaphysics, a sister taxon rather than a variant of anything within it. Logical realism survives the alienness: non-contradiction is preserved because disjoint attributes taken in \emph{intersection} are exactly what the framework prunes as invalid. The blank $e_{ij}$ therefore marks no absent classical flag; it marks the primitive substance operating unimpeded, the stated $e_{ij}$ marking its manufactured suppression. And one division must not blur: \emph{interference}-grade interaction is no property of this attribute-side substance --- it is how \emph{matter} uses the opened jointness, the quantitative layer filling paths into the void the attributes open; or-jointness is an abstract attribute-mediated state, and the interaction is its material use \cite{Aethus}.} Written as algebra, the two forms are
\begin{equation*}
\text{agreeing:}\;\; \bigcup_i a_i \qquad\qquad \text{disagreeing:}\;\; \bigcup_i \Big( a_i \cap \bigcap_{j \neq i} \neg a_j \Big),
\end{equation*}
where the lower-case letters are pegs of one attribute and each term of the disagreeing form is a state whose interior holds one peg and, filling the attribute with exactly that peg, excludes the rest, an interior being always of a state and holding pegs, never other states or their negations; and the placement of the exclusions is the entire content: the non-interactivity that makes the road sign appear on only one side lives in the \emph{interior} of each pocket, never in an exterior wall between pockets. The two sums in play are one filling read under two intersected conditions: the pegs inside a filling are the attribute's open values and say nothing about exclusivity; intersected with the single-peg carving, the filling is the union of the determinate states, the disagreeing form above, its outer union composing states; intersected with the irreducibility marker, its determinate refinements are invalid and it is the agreeing superposition proper \cite{Aethus}. There is no physical ``or'' holding alternatives apart; there is only co-presence, with or without interior negations---which inverts the classical picture, since jointness remains the unobstructed default and mutual exclusivity the manufactured special case, interference being one physical use of that unobstructed jointness.



Jevons's inclusive \emph{or} is the precedent for how a reform of this kind stays invisible: a change at the level of what disjunction is, adopted so completely that the prior reading becomes unthinkable within a generation. The precedent is for the reform's invisibility, not its warrant; the warrant is the removability of exclusion, and the comparisons with quantum logic and with the color-exclusion problem are the exclusivity paper's \cite{Aethus}.



\section{The Postulates, as Codification}\label{sec:postulates}

The reader now holds an ontology, and it is time to say what its laws are. The framing matters: nothing in this section is a new request. The three postulates of the framework are codifications of rules the reader has been using since section~\ref{sec:books}, and the right way to read them is as an inventory of commitments already granted.

\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{P1.}] \emph{Indexing.} Every reality-attribution---every determinate \emph{content}---is of the form ``$q$ takes value $v$ relative to $A$''; there is no $A$-free ledger of content. What is un-indexed is \emph{structure}: the validity functional and the rule from configurations to record partitions, the grammar over vantages that section~\ref{sec:collection} owns as such, which is not itself a vantage. A fact with no \Aethus{} to carry it is not a modest fact but a category error, and the error has an exact shape: truth is not a property statement-objects carry on their own---a stated attribute taken alone is an unapplied thing, an operator dangling without an argument---and what truth \emph{is}, categorically, is the event of application: the attribute meeting an \Aethus's structure, and the structure responding.\footnote{The long paper's image for the same point: truth is one half of a torn fabric whose irreducible other half is the ancestor--descendant structure of a selected \Aethus. ``Actual'' then receives a definition rather than a primitive role: the actual is the state of a stated attribute surviving the pruning (P3 below), when intersected with a given \Aethus; the false, the state of that intersection being invalid. This is the commitment granted the moment the books were opened, which the note of section~\ref{sec:nogo} argues cannot be walked back.}
\item[\textbf{P2.}] \emph{Blanks.} Relative to any valid \Aethus{} $A$: an attribute $q$ is in superposition relative to $A$ if and only if $q$ is blank relative to $A$---blank in the retrieval sense of section~\ref{sec:books}, its entire answer space open, which for an elementary attribute is exactly $A(q)=\ast$ and for a composite is stricter than $A(q)=\ast$ (a mixed composite is unstated without being in superposition). The mixed grade arises in this paper at exactly one place, a reopening's intermediate under a rival reading (section~\ref{sec:deletion}); in every use of the postulate below---the organizer's which-door attribute, the cross attribute $e_{ij}$, the powerset's bins, the entangled pair---the target is elementary or wholly blank, and $A(q)=\ast$ and blank coincide. Blankness \emph{is} superposition, not a pointer to a hidden value---the bookkeeping rule of section~\ref{sec:books} restated as a law, now that \S\ref{sec:nogo} has priced the deflation.\footnote{The extrusion principle's deepest job is enabling this postulate, and the doctrine that job carries---predetermination per \Aethus{}, randomness over Aethae---is promoted from this footnote to its own titled statement, \S\ref{sec:scope}.}
\item[\textbf{P3.}] \emph{Pruning.} An alternative survives only if it can be consistently refined, and alternatives failing this are not improbable but \emph{invalid}: pruned, with no standing in the realizable residue, the tagged component remaining represented beneath its mark. Stated with full care the constraint is a two-quantifier $\exists\forall$ rule over the \emph{proper-descendant} relation: writing $B \le A$ for ``$B$ is a \emph{proper descendant} of $A$''---a \emph{descendant}, resolving any set of $A$'s blanks along its own standing superposition structure ($B = A$ included), its cells terms of $A$'s decomposition at coefficients not measure-zero relative to the rest---the relation is reflexive and transitive, any number of resolutions compacting into one, a proper descendant of a proper descendant being one, and the rule's two quantifiers range over descendants at every depth, not over two rungs of a ladder, the proper-descendant relation being the subrelation of content inclusion $\sqsubseteq$ the postulate ranges over, as fixed among the Terms of \S\ref{sec:intro}. A refinement is the \emph{selection} of an alternative that the graph already offers and affirmatively renders \emph{resolvable}, and that the standing books do not forbid, never the arbitrary insertion of content from outside the structure.\footnote{``Refinement'' throughout this paper means exactly this relation, reflexivity included by design: the resolvability restriction---a refinement selects an alternative the graph affirmatively renders resolvable, as against a mere extension by arbitrary content---is what the long paper's ``proper'' marks, and it is not strictness; the trivial refinement $B = A$ is the formal counterpart of the waiting the derivation below invokes. The deeper characterization is the companion's reduced form, $\bar{A} = \sup\,\{\,B \le A : \forall\, C \le B,\ \mathcal{V}[C]\,\}$, where it exists: P3 requires every valid \Aethus{} to admit a safe proper child, and where the reduced-form construction is established the surviving content admits the corresponding reduced representation; the algebra companion proves, separately, a real-weight maximum for weighted combinations in its specified setting and an order on a parent's partial fillings under stated representation assumptions; their correspondence with the general reduced form is open, so existence of a safe proper child is not by itself existence of the stated supremum. A terminal \Aethus{}---no blanks left that the graph affirmatively renders resolvable---is classified by its own direct consistency, standing as its own reduced form through the reflexive self-refinement.}
Validity then satisfies
\begin{equation*}
\mathcal{V}[A] \;\Longrightarrow\; \exists\, B \le A,\ \forall\, C \le B,\ \mathcal{V}[C],
\end{equation*}
with $\mathcal{V}$ the \emph{largest} predicate disjoint from the base contradictions and satisfying the displayed implication---the greatest fixed point of the safety operator, \S\ref{sec:fixedpoint}, since the implication alone is a necessary condition satisfied by the empty predicate---a $\exists\forall$ alternation whose necessity the long paper derives twice over. Read as a game: the observer refines, nature refines in reply, and $A$ is valid exactly when the observer has a refinement that survives every reply.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Scope Split: Predetermination per \Aethus{}, Randomness over Aethae}\label{sec:scope}

Two scope disciplines govern every claim the postulates make, and both are promoted here from the footnotes they previously occupied, because each is load-bearing enough to be pointed at by name. The first fixes what \emph{predetermination} means and where it holds. Blanks apply to disagreeing as well as agreeing superpositions, and under P2 they are, by construction, constructive---literal reflections of a given \Aethus{}'s reality: to a given \Aethus{}, the block universe it describes holds blanks, standing as superpositions, as literal content within it, the existence-state at each point strictly not undermined by any other point. Looking at a specific block universe, everything conspires under the constraint that it exists---fixing any point freezes it into that block, with the freeze propagating as a constraint to everywhere else therein, the \textit{pre-} naming the proper-time direction along which that consistency is read. The framework then places whole Aethae in disagreeing superposition, and every branch carries equally that much predetermined-ness, fixity of its rendered structure, blanks included, and not a value for every attribute; what Aethic pruning contributes empirically is not more determinism but more visibility, removing joint combinations so that per-branch consistency shows through. \emph{Predetermination per \Aethus{}, randomness over Aethae}: the two claims are never in tension, because they hold at different scopes, and conflating the scopes is precisely what makes the accounting look paradoxical. Delayed choice in Wheeler's family is the structure's special case---what stands in \emph{disagreeing} superposition is the joint interference-with-no-detector case against the joint no-interference-with-detector case, one phenomenon made empirically obvious one joint branch at a time (the quantum-controlled family, where the configuration pockets stand agreeing, is sorted at section~\ref{sec:powerset})---the agreeing superposition of paths standing between determinate nodes in any case, its character never overwritten anywhere in the block.

The second discipline is the split between \emph{measurement} and \emph{observation}: two processes, not one. Measurement is the transfer that asserts a record into the world's own sector---an interaction whose posterity any future index can inherit---while observation is the indexing transit by which a particular \Aethus{} comes to state what the record fixed; the boundary theorem of section~\ref{sec:powerset} classifies by the first, collapse in section~\ref{sec:collection} is the second, and most of the measurement problem's classic confusions are the two processes run together under one word (full development in the companion \cite{Aethus}). Everything downstream should be read against both disciplines, and the satellite papers point here when they invoke either.

\subsection{Where the Third Postulate Comes From}\label{sec:derivation}

P3 deserves its derivation on the page, because ``collapse is derived'' is this paper's sharpest claim and the derivation is short enough to audit. Run the double-slit experiment in a deliberately macroscopic costume: participants are secretly assigned one of two doors and a position to walk to; an organizer sits blindfolded until the end. The organizer's \Aethus{} leaves the which-door attributes blank, so P2 places them in superposition---and yet it is guaranteed that, on removing the blindfold, the organizer sees two clusters and never an interference pattern. This, named, is Schr\"odinger's cat played macroscopically straight: the fate-attribute is blank relative to the outside \Aethus{}, so the superposition stands by the second postulate alone---the semantics' residue operation (section~\ref{sec:perturbation}), with quantum mechanics never consulted---and the macro-scale record supply prunes it to the \emph{disagreeing} kind: exclusive branches, no interference to find. The companion prices the trade without flinching: ordinariness across the macroscopic world is sacrificed---everything classical becomes subtly stranger---to instate realism into quantum mechanics. Why the two clusters, then?

The naive answer---\emph{a future version of the organizer could simply ask a participant, and the answer would contradict the interference pattern, so the interference state is impossible}---fails on inspection, and the failure is instructive. The contradiction occurs in a \emph{future} \Aethus, and P1 forbids exporting contradictions freely between Aethae: if a clash in any one information state could invalidate any other, implication would flow between arbitrary Aethae and an observer-independent background \emph{content} would be smuggled back in through the plumbing. So the puzzle is sharp: the \emph{possibility} of a future contradiction must constrain the present \Aethus{} without violating the framework's own prohibition on arbitrary cross-\Aethus{} traffic.

The derivation proceeds by elimination over the candidate rules. A single-quantifier existential rule---invalid if \emph{some} refinement is invalid---is too strong, and the companion's inheritance diagram makes the failure exact: let a state $A$ hold refinements $B$ and $C$, with $C$ invalid. The rule strikes $A$ through $C$; but invalid inheritance---every refinement of an invalid \Aethus{} is invalid, the one corollary that follows from the definition itself (immediate at the clean-future grade the companion's fixed-point lemma certifies)---then forces $B$ down with its newly condemned parent, while $B$ stands valid by construction, healthy refinements and all. One bad option among many condemns its innocent siblings, which is the collision stated directionally: invalidity flows from a state to its refinements, never from a refinement to the state \cite{Aethus}. A single-quantifier universal rule---invalid only if \emph{all} refinements are contradictions---is too weak: the organizer's state always has refinements that are not yet contradictions, since one can simply wait, so the rule never fires; worse, those waiting-states would have to ground each other's invalidity in a circle with no base case. What breaks the impasse is a structural observation: the problematic states are \emph{self-similar}---every non-contradictory refinement of the organizer's state is another state of the same kind, facing the same inescapable menu. Folding that self-similarity into the existential fact that each such state has \emph{some} contradictory refinement yields a two-quantifier alternation, and generalizing gives P3 exactly as displayed above.
(Elimination over three candidates is elimination only if they exhaust the field, and the field is the unweighted quantifier-prefix rules on the refinement relation---fixed as such by the division of labor section~\ref{sec:deletion} states, validity binary and weight graded, which excludes any rule keyed to the weight of contradiction-reaching refinements and therefore stands among the elimination's premises; P3's \emph{invalid, not improbable} clause restates that division rather than supplying it. Within that field a rule is fixed by its first quantifier and its second, each ranging over refinements, which are descendants at every depth. At the first quantifier an existential is excluded by the inheritance direction---a rule that condemns a parent through one descendant condemns that descendant's innocent siblings, whatever they hold deeper---so that quantifier is universal or the rule is wrong; at the second a universal never fires, since waiting is always a refinement, so that quantifier is existential or the rule is idle. That leaves the alternation displayed, and it is already closed: read over descendants at every depth, ``some refinement all of whose refinements are safe'' reaches the whole cone in one application, so no deeper nesting adds anything it does not contain, (section~\ref{sec:fixedpoint}).)
The chess reading is then literal rather than decorative: \emph{check} is the escapable possibility of contradiction; \emph{checkmate} is the impossibility of escaping that possibility; and checkmate is invalidity. The postulate says: it must not be impossible to make contradiction impossible. The organizer's interference-\Aethus{} is checkmated---every refinement leaves which-door information retrievable in principle, by asking, by records, by entropic traces---while the two-cluster alternative has a clean future and survives. Collapse, in this costume, is a theorem about which bookkeeping states have escape routes.
The column discipline of section~\ref{sec:coupling} binds this claim too. What the elimination derives is the \emph{form}, the two-quantifier alternation; the base contradiction it runs on---that a refinement stating both the interference and a which-door value is invalid---is supplied by the union move's identification of interference with record-absence (section~\ref{sec:union}), a posit of the ontology rather than an output of the elimination, without which the organizer's interference-state has no contradictory refinement to find. ``Collapse is derived'' reads at that grade: form from the elimination, trigger from the union move---Feynman's rule formalized, which section~\ref{sec:powerset} collects as a deliverable.
\subsection{The Fixed-Point Form}\label{sec:fixedpoint}

P3 has a standard mathematical shape, and naming it is what plugs the collapse law into an existing, well-understood genre---though, as the paragraph ends by saying, the shape closes at once and nothing in it recurses. Collect the non-recursive failures---direct contradictions among stated attributes, P2 violations---into a set $\mathrm{Base}$, and define the \emph{doomed} Aethae as the least fixed point
\begin{equation*}
\mathfrak{D} \;=\; \mu X.\big(\, \mathrm{Base} \,\cup\, \{\, A \;:\; \forall B \le A,\ \exists C \le B,\ C \in X \,\}\,\big),
\end{equation*}
so that an \Aethus{} is doomed when every proper descendant has a doomed further one. Here $\le$ is \emph{declared descent}, the splittings each \Aethus{} admits, and not the lattice order, which would place the tagged all-statements \Aethus{} beneath everything: an attribute whose single-peg carving the \Aethus{} holds splits into its values, a filling asserted without one declares no split, and an irreducible agreeing superposition splits only when a record grants access, into children that are all tagged. Stated so, and with finitely many attributes and the algebra paper's closure rule, that a carrier holding a carving each of whose settings is tagged is itself tagged, doom coincides with closure's own tag and the doomed Aethae form a multiplicative ideal, so that validity is an exact quotient of the algebra \cite{Aethus}; beyond finitely many attributes the coincidence is open. Validity is the complement of $\mathfrak{D}$: the \emph{greatest} fixed point of the dual safety operator. This is precisely the shape of a safety property in the modal $\mu$-calculus, and of the non-losing positions in a game against nature---which the chess idiom names, though as an idiom only: membership in $D$ says that every declared descendant has a tagged descendant, which is weaker than every continuation meeting a tag in finitely many steps, and finite branching supplies no finite certificate; finite termination holds separately in models with finitely many attributes, where each declared split fixes an attribute for good. The Knaster--Tarski theorem guarantees both fixed points exist for arbitrary branching, so nothing here presupposes finiteness; and because declared descent is reflexive and transitive---a proper descendant of a proper descendant is one, any number of resolutions compacting into one---the iteration closes at its first stage for any branching whatever, so that P3's own $\exists\forall$ statement, read over descendants at every depth, is the fixed point rather than a seed for computing it: a state is valid exactly when some descendant has a cone free of base contradictions. The equivalence between P3 as stated and this fixed-point formulation, under the base-generation reading of invalidity, is stated and proven as a lemma in the long paper \cite{Aethus}; the short version's job is only to display the shape. The least fixed point also carries the framework's own recursive reading on its face, its quantifiers ranging over every declared depth at once, and, as noted just above, finite branching supplies no finite certificate: a finitely branching waiting chain with a contradictory exit at every stage has every position with access to contradiction while an infinite waiting continuation never takes the exit. Finite termination holds separately where each declared split fixes an attribute for good and the attributes are finitely many.

Validity and the \emph{reduced form} are then the engine. The reduced form of an \Aethus{} $A$ is defined as the supremum, by Aethic parentage, of the set of proper children of $A$ all of whose own proper children are valid---informally, the maximal safely-refinable core, the residue of alternatives surviving the pruning. Three results bear on it and are not to be run together: the order of a parent's partial fillings, under its representation assumptions; the maximum attained by real-weight combinations, their valid-support truncation; and the correspondence of that maximum with this supremum, which the algebra companion books as open \cite{AethusTree}. P3 requires every valid \Aethus{} to admit a safe proper child; where the reduced-form construction is established, that is the statement that the surviving content admits its reduced representation. Two facts give the definition its standing. First, an equivalence: by the Aethic Partition Theorem of the long paper, the union of all elements of a partition equals the union of its valid elements alone, since invalid Aethae contribute zero weight (weight-zero being the algebraic face of the invalidity label, which the term keeps, \S\ref{sec:close})---so the reduced form is Aethically indistinguishable from the original at the tier that matters, agreement of realizable content. Pruning discards nothing that was ever available to be inhabited. Second, every operative notion of the paper has already been defined over it: a counterfactual's verdict is read off it, a dependence is checked by whether one perturbation changes it, and weight lives on it. One property, before the derivation: the pruning is driven by \emph{stated information}---records, correlations, anything an \Aethus{} carries---and by nothing else. Not size, not mass, not the presence of anyone's gaze. That austerity is about to do the work.

\section{One Operation, Many Phenomena: The Reduced-Form Coupling}\label{sec:coupling}

When the pruning eliminates \emph{joint} configurations of several attributes, the survivors exhibit a correlation that nobody put in by hand: certain values of one attribute now only ever occur alongside certain values of another, not because anything signaled anything, but because the combinations that would have broken the correlation were crossed out. Call this \emph{reduced form coupling}. The framework's compression claim---that phenomena standardly treated as unrelated are one mechanism---rests on this operation, so it should be watched running on the humblest possible case before it is claimed for entanglement. Every step below is the same operation later run under quantum weights.

Take the Monty Hall problem, in one paragraph, since its result is placement and not evidence. Write $B_i$ for \emph{the car is behind door $i$} and $O_j$ for \emph{the host opens door $j$}; the uniform prior is the weighted \Aethus{} $A_{\textnormal{orig}} = \tfrac13 B_1 + \tfrac13 B_2 + \tfrac13 B_3$, the contestant committed to Door~1 by convention. The host's conditional-response rule is itself an Aethic object---the \emph{Monty Hall Nexus}, to this problem what the Quantum Nexus of section~\ref{sec:nexus} is to the physics---and with the pick stated its survivor is $X = \tfrac12 (O_2 + O_3) B_1 + O_3 B_2 + O_2 B_3$, best read by its \emph{absences}: $O_1$ appears nowhere (the host never opens the picked door); $O_2 B_2$ and $O_3 B_3$ appear nowhere (never the car door). Those joint configurations are Aethic contradictions that never enter the structure---the host's ``choice'' realized as an invalidation mechanism, not a dynamical influence---and the lone $\tfrac12$ marks the one branch where the host retains an option. \section{The Climax: The Boundary Is Informational}\label{sec:powerset}

Take a system with enumerable alternatives---the laboratory case will be a particle with available paths---and instead of considering the alternatives one at a time, consider the \emph{powerset}: every combination of alternatives held together, each combination a candidate pocket of co-present content. The framework's constraints now act on the powerset, and the theorem that organizes everything downstream is this:

Fix the laboratory case: $n$ slits and an arbitrary configuration of detectors, each detector firing on traversal of any slit in its designated subset.
Everything that follows is indexed, per P1: the configuration is stated content relative to the \Aethus{} $A$ doing the classifying, and the partition and the bins are $A$'s. Where detector-presence itself stands blank relative to $A$, the classification runs pocket-wise over $A$'s configuration pockets, one partition per pocket, and the criterion of section~\ref{sec:union} sorts the delayed-choice literature into its two families. Where the setting was chosen and recorded and only $A$ has not learned it, the pockets are \emph{disagreeing}: one partition obtains, unread---Wheeler's case \cite{wheeler1983law}. Where the setting is carried by a quantum control uncorrelated with any path record \cite{ionicioiu2011proposal}, the pockets are \emph{agreeing}: the partition itself stands in agreeing superposition, and no fact of which obtains is available to be unread---the wave--particle intermediates those experiments report. How the cross-partition terms are carried is the weighting's business, stated at \S\ref{sec:two_tag_brief}; the family is previewed here only so that it is seen to be handled, and the theorem uses none of that machinery. What is \emph{not} indexed is the rule from a stated configuration to its partition, the shared grammar of every vantage, which section~\ref{sec:collection} owns as such.
Throughout, a \emph{detector} is a device whose marker states for distinct slits are orthogonal, so that its signature is deterministic; overlapping markers are the kernel's business (\S\ref{sec:two_tag_brief}). Assign each slit its \emph{detector signature}---the tuple recording which detectors would fire if that slit alone were traversed. Slits with identical signatures are indistinguishable by every record the signature includes; that no record, present or future, separates them is the completeness of the signature for the declared configuration, stipulated below, and that the separation persists through every proper descendant is a second stipulation, persistence, distinct from the first. The signatures therefore partition the slits into equivalence classes $E_1, \dots, E_k$, and the theorem classifies every element of the powerset against that partition.

\begin{theorem}[Powerset Invalidation]\label{thm:powerset-short} Assume the declared record structure is complete and persistent: the signatures include all relevant accessible records, and every proper descendant retains a descendant stating a separating record whenever the candidate spans signature classes. Then every subset $T$ of the $n$ slits---every candidate claim ``the particle traverses exactly these slits in coherent superposition''---falls, relative to an \Aethus{} in which the detector configuration is stated and over the specified candidate domain, into exactly one of three bins: (i) \emph{valid}: $T$ is empty or equals one full equivalence class; (ii) \emph{over-specified}, invalid by record-individuation: $T$ is a nonempty proper subset of a single class, drawing a distinction that no information could ever support; (iii) \emph{third-postulate invalid}: $T$ spans two or more classes, combining alternatives some record can distinguish, so that a future refinement can expose the maintained coherence as contradictory---checkmate, in the sense of section~\ref{sec:fixedpoint}. The bins are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive, with the counts explicit: $k$ valid nonempty cases, $\sum_j (2^{|E_j|} - 2)$ over-specified cases, and the remainder third-postulate.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\emph{Proof.} For nonempty $T$, the signature partition gives an elementary trichotomy: $T$ is a proper subset of one class; $T$ equals one class; $T$ meets two or more classes. In the first case $T$ distinguishes slits that share every possible record, so by record-individuation no attribute carries the distinction and, over the specified candidate domain, whose preparation fixes the classes, the claim over-specifies. The bin rests on record-individuation and that domain, not on P2 alone: the framework recognizes mixed, partially constrained attributes, so a proper part of a class is not excluded merely for falling short of a whole answer space; the companion's preparation-relative classification is the version followed here \cite{AethusQuant}. In the second, every branch of the maintained superposition produces the identical total signature, so no refinement along accessible records can expose a contradiction, and the state has a clean future---valid (empty $T$ trivially so; with no detectors, $k = 1$ and the sole valid nonempty case is the full set). In the third, some detector's record separates a slit of $T$ in one class from a slit in another, so the coherence claim conjoined with that record's future stating is contradictory while the record family is persistent over the candidate's declared descent, every refinement having a further descendant stating the separating record, which is the persistence hypothesis and not completeness, completeness fixing only what the signature includes---checkmate in the fixed-point sense above, third-postulate invalid. Exclusivity and exhaustiveness are the trichotomy's own, and the counts follow by enumeration. \hfill$\square$

\noindent Children, fixed: what counts as a refinement of the classifying state is determined by the configuration that state itself states, along its signature partition---so in the no-detector configuration the singleton resolutions inside a class are not refinements of any state, and P3, which quantifies over refinements, never reaches them; the axiomatization companion states this as its Lemma (Children in a configuration), and without it the paradigm valid state would be doomed by its own bin-(ii) singletons \cite{AethusQuant}.

\noindent A corollary worth one sentence: moving or erasing a detector re-draws the signature partition and re-bins the powerset---the sliding quantum--classical boundary as literal re-classification. And the theorem's shape in one breath: bookkeeping over a partition plus two postulates, with everything quantum entering through which partition the records induce.
