\noindent Philosophy of science has studied discovery mostly through third-party reconstructions from the notebooks of the dead; prospective, self-deposited, real-time records with public certificates have been scarce, because the discipline's founding hygiene --- the separation of the context of discovery from the context of justification --- hardened into a publication genre that discards such data at the source. The position taken on the old firewall is split and exact: dropped for content, because discovery context is evidence; re-erected for normativity, because there are no rules of discovery, while the rules of checking --- exhibited here in an AI-era provenance apparatus --- should be exacting.

\section{The claim, and a disclosure}\label{sec:claim}

The discovery side of science is not unstudied --- the notebooks of the dead have been read closely and well: Gruber on Darwin \cite{gruber1974darwin}, Tweney on Faraday \cite{tweney1985faraday}, Holmes on Krebs \cite{holmes1991krebs}, Nersessian on Maxwell \cite{nersessian2008creating}, the discovery-logic debates collected by Nickles \cite{nickles1980discovery}, and Hanson before all of them \cite{hanson1958patterns}. What the field has scarcely possessed is data of a different kind: prospective, self-deposited, real-time records kept by the discoverer while the discovering happened, with public certificates a reader can recompute.\footnote{Two bodies of work come nearest, and each locates the scarcity by what it lacks. Dunbar recorded scientific reasoning as it happened, an observer attending the weekly meetings of molecular-biology and immunology laboratories for a year \cite{dunbar1995how, dunbar1997how}: real-time, but recorded by a third party and confined to the group stage. The Polymath projects put mathematical discovery in progress online in the participants' own timestamped comments, dead ends included \cite{gowers2009massively, polymath2012new}: real-time and self-deposited, but public from the first move, on problems posed in public, the first of them settled in weeks. Neither holds one discoverer's private stages across years, which is what the present record adds, its private leg dated by its author (\S\ref{sec:record}).} The shortage of \emph{that} is self-inflicted. The founding hygiene --- Reichenbach's separation of the context of discovery from the context of justification \cite{reichenbach1938experience}, with Popper's dictum that there is no logical method of having new ideas as its normative twin \cite{popper1959logic} --- was adopted so thoroughly that it hardened into a publication genre: finished papers present finished structures, the scaffolding is struck before any reader is allowed inside, and the records of how a framework actually came to be are treated as private exhaust rather than as evidence. Theories of scientific production thus get built from completed artifacts and third-party reconstruction, and features of the artwork are projected onto the making. The present dataset's delta over the notebook studies is access --- real-time rather than reconstructed, self-annotated rather than inferred, certified rather than curated --- and its cost, conceded up front, is worse selection bias: one subject, self-selected, self-reported. I will argue below that this projection is the central category error in the theory of inductive reasoning; but before arguing it, I want to name the evidential situation it has produced, because this paper is constructed specifically to improve that situation from an unusual position: I happen to possess the data.

This paper puts two things on the table. The first is a set of named criteria and theses --- the Strong Implication Criterion and the Strong Rooting Criterion (§\ref{sec:criteria}); the substance/reasoning axis on which failed and successful theories separate (§\ref{sec:axis}); the distinction between ontological and methodological revolutions (§\ref{sec:kinds}); the two-stage, charming-then-structural shape of inference-driven breakthroughs (§\ref{sec:stages}); the shades-of-similarity account of why the deepest unknowns hide (§\ref{sec:shades}); and the thesis that inductive reasoning is an art form of which deductive reasoning is part of the artwork (§\ref{sec:art}). Each is stated in a displayed box so that it can be cited, challenged, or used without archaeology, and each is argued first on external historical material --- Newton and Maxwell, Einstein's road to general relativity, Feynman's nanotechnology lecture, string theory, superdeterminism --- before it is permitted anywhere near my own work. The historical tellings those cases rest on are themselves contested, and nothing here pretends otherwise: the readings used are the standard ones, chosen for legibility, and where a rival historiography reads an episode differently --- Newton's synthesis as more collective and more gradual than the telling here, for one --- the thesis is owed the harder reading rather than the convenient one.\footnote{The standard to aim at is the one \S\ref{sec:retrodiction} sets against itself: cases coded, coding weaknesses named, corrections forced against material already installed. The classic-case treatments are vignettes by comparison --- illustrations calibrated against standard tellings, not independent historical results --- and a reader who rejects a telling should read the corresponding thesis as owing a better case, not as refuted by the disagreement.} The second is a dataset: the dated genealogy of one framework's construction, mine, running from a Google Doc written at 7:55 PM on July 24, 2018, through real-time journal entries of August 2022 and February 2023 reproduced verbatim in §\ref{sec:genealogy}, to four public prints (January and May 2023, 2024, 2026) whose contents --- down to word frequencies --- are checkable by any reader against deposited text. The theses are then run live on the dataset, and the reader is invited to audit the fit rather than take my word for it.

Now the disclosure, because the paper's structure makes it mandatory rather than polite. Every thesis defended here classifies my own framework favorably: on my own axis my framework falls on the reasoning side; in my own taxonomy of revolutions it is of the ontological kind; my own genealogy exhibits the two-stage shape my own model predicts. That is an occupational hazard of doing philosophy of science from inside a research program, and I would rather name it than perform innocence of it. The mitigations are structural. The external cases come first, and the theses must earn their keep there before touching anything of mine. The dataset is dated, deposited, and checkable --- including its second column, the things the early records got wrong or dressed differently, which I have kept on the page rather than pruned. And the theses are stated sharply enough to fail: a reader who finds a substance-adding theory that succeeded, a deep unknown that arrived wearing no camouflage, or an axiomatization of inductive practice that did not smuggle its era's assumptions has found a counterexample, and I have written the boxes so that such a reader knows exactly what they have hit.

There is a second disclosure inside the first, and it changes the logic of the hazard rather than merely its optics: for the two criteria of §\ref{sec:criteria} --- and, per the genealogy's own earliest exhibit, for the axis in embryo --- the direction of dependence runs from the methodological intuitions to the framework, not the reverse. The intuitions came first --- the dissatisfaction with superdeterminism, the rooting instinct that the constraint must live one layer up --- and the framework was deliberately constructed to land them: built to the spec, not scored by a curve fitted afterward, which is what §\ref{sec:criteria}'s compass sentence already asserts in passing and is here asserted in full. That dissolves the post-hoc charge at a price this paper accepts explicitly: a construction engineered to pass a test contributes nothing to the test's validity, so my framework's favorable scores are zero evidence for the criteria, which must earn their entire keep on the external cases --- the external-cases-first structure above is that logic enforced, not a courtesy. What the design direction buys is the converse reading: the scores report that the construction met its target, and they carry exactly as much weight as the criteria independently earn. The priority claim is itself dateable and graded like everything else here --- certified where the intuitions' early statements survive in dated entries, memoir-grade where they do not. And one thesis stands exempt from the charge of design by its nature: the two-stage shape of my own genealogy cannot be a design artifact, because being surprised by one's own future self is not a specification whose satisfaction anyone can engineer. Any attentive reader will complete the syllogism, and I will not perform not having completed it myself. One more declaration, of genre. Unlike the framework extractions that share this corpus, this essay is deliberately first-person, and the choice is methodological rather than stylistic. The memoir-as-data method has a small but venerable lineage --- Einstein's autobiographical notes, Poincar\'e's essays on invention \cite{poincare1908science}, Hadamard's study of mathematical creation \cite{hadamard1945invention}, Polanyi's personal knowledge \cite{polanyi1958personal} --- and what that lineage understood is that the first-person record is not a rhetorical liability to be laundered out but the only place certain evidence exists at all. My position on the old firewall is therefore split, and I want to state it exactly: I drop the discovery/justification separation for \emph{content}, because the context of discovery is evidence and discarding evidence is not rigor; and I re-erect it for \emph{normativity}, because there are no rules of discovery worth the name, while the rules of checking should be exacting. The paper practices both halves: the genealogy below is as unguarded as the original notebooks, and every claim that can be checked is routed to something datable, deposited, or recomputable.\footnote{Reflexivity is not new to the study of science, and the positioning is owed. The sociology of scientific knowledge made it a principle --- the Strong Programme's fourth tenet \cite{bloor1976knowledge} --- and Ashmore's study performed its own thesis on the page \cite{ashmore1989reflexive}, a wing whose textual self-reference famously spiraled toward regress, which is part of why the mainstream kept both its distance and its self-exemption. What this paper practices is reflexivity of a different kind: certificate-bearing rather than literary --- self-application that terminates in recomputations over frozen deposits and at an independent gate, rather than in further text about itself --- so the regress that made reflexivity a cautionary tale finds no purchase here, and the mainstream's reason for abstaining does not transfer.}

Against: correlation is shared risk --- these members issue from one mind and one framework, so damage propagates, and ten interlocked claims are nearer to one structure with ten load-bearing members than to ten independent results; a referee who wounds a member should expect the wound to travel, and is invited to test how far. For: coherence is audit efficiency --- any member can be tested against the others' stated constraints, and a structure whose parts visibly object when one is removed cannot be dismissed claim-by-claim without the objections going on the record. Let $D$ be discovery-record evidence, $H_P$ a hypothesis about the process by which a theory arose, and $H_T$ the hypothesis that the theory is true. The split firewall says: $P(H_P \mid D)$ may legitimately differ from $P(H_P)$---the record is evidence about the process, about what was written and thought when, about whether idea $A$ preceded idea $B$, about whether a later reconstruction matches the contemporary notes---while $D$ moves $P(H_T)$ only through what it shows about the process. The principle must not be stated one notch too strong, and the correct strength is this: $D$ is directly evidential about $H_P$, and $D$ does not confirm $H_T$ \emph{merely because the discovery narrative has a favored shape}. It is not that process evidence can never bear on truth. A record can show that a theory was fitted blind before a held-out dataset was seen, that an apparent derivation was reverse-engineered from its answer, that independent routes converged on one result, or that reporting was selective---and each is a fact about the process that rationally moves confidence in $H_T$ with no newly precommitted prediction, because each bears on the reliability of the method that produced the theory. What the firewall forbids is the direct channel, from the shape of the story to the truth of its conclusion. Everything the dataset of §\ref{sec:record} is asked to show below is a question of the first kind, and the paper's criteria receive no confirmation from that dataset in the second, as the disclosure above already said in other words.

\section{Two criteria of adequacy}\label{sec:criteria}

I begin with the two criteria, because they are the sharpest tools in the kit and because their first statements arose in anger --- specifically, in my dissatisfaction with superdeterminism, which will serve as the running negative example.

\begin{criterion}{The Strong Implication Criterion}
An ontological model which consists with phenomena but does not so much as imply them is itself incomplete (the weak form). The strong form adds: one should not be able to switch the empiricism out for distinct alternatives and fit the base form of the model just as easily. Visually, a model should be wound so tightly to the empiricism that shifting the empiricism from what is known would dissatisfy the model immediately, while not being so hastily thrown together that subtracting empirical knowns would undermine its fit --- a cone: sharp focus, wide and thoughtful base.
\end{criterion}

% >>> FIXED (R860, Oct 26 2026): Mercury's perihelion follows from the field equations of general relativity, not from general covariance (Norton 1993 added).

The strong form is a substitution test, and a substitution test is only as sharp as its three parameters, which the criterion as boxed leaves to the reader: which alternative empiricisms are in play, what counts as preserving the model's base form, and what counts as fitting just as easily. Left open, the criterion can be run after the fact on anything; so it is given a protocol. Let $C$ be a theoretical core, $\mathcal{A}$ an auxiliary class with a stated complexity measure, $E$ the actual empirical structure, and $\mathcal{E}$ a \emph{declared} contrast family of alternative empiricisms, fixed before the test. Define the substitution set $S_C := \{E' \in \mathcal{E} : \exists A' \in \mathcal{A},\ \mathrm{complexity}(A') \le \mathrm{complexity}(A),\ (C, A') \text{ fits } E'\}$. The strong form then says that an adequate model makes $S_C$ small relative to $\mathcal{E}$: the core with auxiliaries no heavier than the ones actually fielded should fit few of the declared alternatives. A critic can now run the test, and a proponent can now fail it. Superdeterminism is the clean failure case. Suppose a universe in which superdeterminism accurately describes the quantum effect --- the Bell correlations implied by an underlying deterministic phenomenon. I then ask why we are to see the quantum phenomenon in the form in which we do at all. Why the wavefunction? Why superpositions? Why the uncertainty principle? Why does any particular quantum effect appear rather than not appear? On the surface this looks like a nitpick; in fact it is the entire question. Superdeterminism does not feign to discriminate between the quantum presentation and a myriad of alternative presentations the same deterministic substrate could equally have worn, and so it fails the weak form and the strong form together. The positive contrast is instructive: general relativity does not merely permit the perihelion behavior of Mercury --- its field equations entail it,\footnote{The field equations, and not general covariance alone: covariance constrains the form of a theory, and its physical content has been disputed since Kretschmann \cite{norton1993general}.} so the theory could not survive that behavior's absence, and could not have been refit, unchanged at its base, to a modestly different gravitational phenomenology. That tightness, not mere consistency, is what an ontological proposal owes. The criterion has a near relative in the requirement that good explanations be hard to vary \cite{deutsch2011beginning}, and the kinship is acknowledged; the delta is the strong form's substitution test, which quantifies over alternative empiricisms rather than over variants of the explanation. And one Duhemian caveat is owed \cite{duhem1954aim}: the criterion is assessed against a model together with its auxiliaries, never against a naked core --- what must fail under substituted empiricism is the bundle actually fielded. And the delta, per the record, is load-bearing rather than taxonomic. Run as a compass at first contact, the substitution test convicted classical reductionism in real time: a universe in which the quantum reduces \emph{efficiently} to the classical on demand makes no discrimination between quantum-computational speedups being permitted and being forbidden --- and the universe is specific, the speedups are permitted --- so at least one quantum feature must resist the reduction.\footnote{Efficiently: what the speedups exclude is efficient classical simulation, not a classical ontology as such, since ontological models of quantum theory can carry exponential resource costs \cite{montina2008exponential}.} That inference arrived during the introductory double-slit video itself, in August 2022: the account stands attested in the 2024 deposit, its statement thereby predating this paper; the dated August 22 entries sit twelve days downstream, the account's ``within the week'' chaining video to eureka and §\ref{sec:stages_annotated}'s own within-the-week chaining eureka to entry, the two short hops closing cleanly across the gap; and the day-precision, August 10, was read off the author's YouTube watch history in a lookup some two years downstream --- a platform-dated log rather than bare recollection, the lookup's report the author's own, with an export or capture of the entry the outstanding step that completes the certificate. Its conclusion is the shape the nonclassicality literature took decades to distill: most of the quantum phenomenology reproducible under classical epistemic restriction, genuine nonclassicality concentrated in what Spekkens has called the ``thin film'' --- the remainder where computational advantage lives \cite{spekkens2007evidence, spekkens2024escapedsapiens}. The second criterion needs its target on the page first, because it was built against a specific standing doctrine: \emph{quantum logical antirealism}, the common stance that the quantum world is not merely undescribed but rationally indescribable. The doctrine grades into three orders. First-order: each deeper discovery is intrinsically more impersonal than the last, as if by an ontological trajectory --- a falloff between physics and one's tangible life. Second-order: human intuitions and mental capabilities prove insufficient for quantum superposition specifically. Third-order --- full-blown --- is the Bohr-flavored claim that no tangible logical system can encapsulate quantum effects at all. The load-bearing distinction the doctrine elides is between \emph{a lack of ontological applicability for the standard rational system} and \emph{a lack for any rational system at all}: the former is just the pre-revolutionary condition, while the latter is what the criterion below convicts of paradox. The first order, at least, admits a clean psychological diagnosis: it is the predictable consequence of \emph{a paradigm's epistemology significantly outpacing its ontology}, and nothing more. Had Planck's quanta gone fifteen years without Einstein's ontological interpretation, the construct would have grown mystical to the point of perceived inaccessibility; had gravitational lensing been detected before general relativity existed, it would have read as a defiance of human reason. In each counterfactual the physics is unchanged and the ``antirealism'' tracks only our temporary lack of ontology --- and the species has run the real experiment: the pre-Newtonian planets, pulled along their orbits by invisible angels, were exactly perceived epistemic antirealism, dissolved not by argument but by the arrival of an axiomatic framework. That precedent was my driving intuition at the very beginning: \emph{it has already been done, by Newton himself, so we know at least that it can be done.}

\begin{criterion}{The Strong Rooting Criterion, in two parts}
\emph{The method (load-bearing):} when a standing framework leaves its subject matter unformulable in any human-understood model while the subject matter plainly exists, the conflict is an instruction, not a paradox --- abstract to the next layer and relocate the theoretical constraint there. \emph{The existence thesis (contestable, recorded):} a physical circumstance that cannot be formulated in the basis of a human-understood model --- ``cannot'' read in principle, under every extension of human-understood modeling, not merely at present --- is not existent but genuinely contradictory.
\end{criterion}

The second part is my metaphysical commitment, stated so it can be contested: it will draw verificationist and idealist charges, and I record my defense in one sentence rather than a section --- this is not ``shut up and calculate'' but a claim about existence, that a universe detachable from its theoretic architecture is not merely unknowable but contradictory, an unphysical human construct disguising itself as a philosophy --- while noting that a reader who rejects the thesis outright loses nothing of the method or of what follows from it, save that derivation, whose separability step leans on the thesis's strongest reading.

One compatibility deserves immediate note: the criterion does not conflict with G\"odel's incompleteness theorem, because an inability to construct a completely descriptive mathematical system is not the prospect of describing an ontological paradigm --- incompleteness constrains the information accessible \emph{within} a paradigm, and says nothing about the ability to establish the paradigm in the first place. Properly denying epistemic antirealism is exactly what lets the counterintuitive logical barriers --- incompleteness, uncertainty --- be partitioned into their correct ontological stances rather than melted together; the Jaynes omelette, part realities of Nature and part incomplete human information scrambled up together \cite{jaynes1990probability}, is the standing image of what the failure to partition looks like. The rooting criterion earns its keep through its contrapositive, which functions as a discovery engine, and the engine is best exhibited on the case that mattered to me. The derivational black box of the Copenhagen interpretation fails the criterion --- quantum logical antirealism leaves the quantum circumstance unformulable in any human-understood model --- and yet quantum mechanics is physically existent. This conflict yields not a paradox but a direct instruction: abstract the informational context of quantum mechanics to a higher level, and constrain \emph{that} theoretically instead. Upon recognizing that unknowing is a fundamental property of the universe, one ought not stop there and declare the universe logically unfounded, for that is exactly the rooting violation; one must abstract to the next layer and understand that the logical dynamics of the universe do not constrain ignorance versus knowledge --- what can be unknown versus known --- but rather constrain the \emph{concept} of ignorance: the structural interrelation within ignorance, treating the knowledge of ignorance itself as a kind of knowledge and constraining it relationally.

I take this to be exactly analogous to what Newton realized about motion. Velocity is relative; rather than concluding that the universe has no laws of motion --- the rooting violation of his day --- Newton inferred that the dynamical constraint attaches not to velocity versus motionlessness but to the abstract study of \emph{change} in velocity. Rooting the constraint one layer up is Newton's second law. The constraint on the dynamics of conceptual ignorance, arrived at by the same move one layer up from the ignorance/knowledge distinction, is the second postulate of the framework developed in the companion manuscript \cite{Aethus}, and this is why I regard that postulate as the counterpart in epistemology of what Newton's second law is in motion --- a claim I state here and argue there. For present purposes the point is methodological: the criterion did not merely evaluate a finished proposal; run backwards, it dictated where the next theory had to live. A criterion that can do both jobs --- filter and compass --- is what this paper means by an adequacy criterion worth naming.

One boundary of the criterion deserves stating, because it is where speculation gets its license back. The rooting criterion does not say speculation is unproductive; it says that alluding outward to some vague deeper principle in order to resolve current problems is problematic in excess (``a future version of superdeterminism will resolve the paradoxes''), while establishing accurate broader features of a future theory without alluding to the manner of its completion tends to be healthy (``the issues in current quantum interpretations are categorically like mistakes in Sudoku, pointing to an inconsistency of our own assumptions''). The first outsources the debt; the second maps it.

The two criteria, finally, are not siblings but a lineage, with rooting the more fundamental --- and the relation admits what the long manuscript calls a toy derivation, migrated here because the defense of §\ref{sec:shape} leans on relations of exactly this kind. Suppose a universe that violates the implication criterion: superdeterminism as its ontology, quantum mechanics as its empiricism, the fit comfortable. Such a universe has locked in a feature --- the theory sits at arm's length from the empiricism, since other empiricisms would fit the same ontology equally well. But that reassignability just is separability of ontology from empiricism, and separability is what the rooting criterion forbids on its strongest reading, which carries a premise the criterion does not state: that a circumstance's theoretic architecture fixes its empirical structure, beyond initial conditions and other auxiliary features, finely enough that no other empiricism fits it as well. On that reading a physical circumstance whose theoretic architecture can be detached from it is not underdescribed but contradictory; without the premise, several intelligible worlds could share one ontology while differing in initial conditions or auxiliaries, and the step fails. Roll the contradiction back and, on that reading, the conclusion follows: a universe violating the implication criterion cannot exist in the first place. A toy derivation, and marked as one --- the quantifiers are informal, and the separability step rests on the premise just stated, which the rooting criterion alone does not supply --- but it is a derivation and not an analogy, and on that premise it converts this section's two criteria into one commitment viewed at two depths.

\section{Installed versus independently entailed structure: the substance/reasoning axis}\label{sec:axis}

\begin{criterion}{The substance/reasoning axis}
Failed theories tend to add new substance to the universe. Successful theories tend to add new reasoning to the universe.\footnotemark
\end{criterion}
\footnotetext{Stated here exactly as the long manuscript states it, because the concision is the point: the axis is meant to be carried in the head, not consulted. The precision gloss, which this section's cases then use: failed theories add substance \emph{installed to rescue standing reasoning}; where successful theories add substance, it is \emph{entailed by the new reasoning} rather than installed to save the old --- the direction-of-dependence formulation on which the apparent counterexamples sort themselves. The gloss is the axis's real content, and it deserves the name that says so: \emph{installed structure} against \emph{independently entailed structure}. Without an operational reading the taxonomy is retrospective---every successful posit becomes ``reasoning'' by saying it was entailed, every failed one ``substance'' by saying it was installed---so the temporal order is made the criterion. A posit $P$ is \emph{independently entailed} if the constraints from which $P$ follows were fixed before, or independently of, the anomaly $D$ it addresses; $P$ is \emph{installed} if its only evidential role is introduced after $D$, specifically to absorb $D$'s residual, while adding no independently exposed consequence. Read that way the axis is decidable from dated records, which is what makes the dataset of §\ref{sec:record} bear on the criterion itself rather than only illustrate it.}

The historical record sorts along this axis with striking regularity. The substance column: epicycles, caloric, phlogiston, the luminiferous ether --- each a new posited \emph{thing}, installed to make the standing reasoning survive contact with the phenomena. The reasoning column: statistical mechanics, which added no new substance to thermodynamics but re-derived it as counting; special relativity, which removed the ether and reorganized simultaneity; natural selection, which added no vital substance and subtracted teleology. On the direction-of-dependence formulation the apparent counterexamples sort themselves: the atomic hypothesis, germ theory, quarks, and the neutrino were all substance \emph{entailed} --- atoms by the counting that re-derived thermodynamics, the neutrino by conservation, quarks by the symmetry classification --- while epicycles, caloric, and ether were substance \emph{installed}, patches whose entire office was rescuing the standing reasoning from the phenomena. The axis is still not a law; I state it as it behaves: a strong prior with a diagnostic edge. When a proposal's central move is a new primitive, the burden of proof it faces is categorically heavier, and the reason can be given structurally. A new primitive connects to the existing theory-graph by essentially one edge --- the introduction itself --- and when we look at the actual universe, every last scientific revolution has revealed it to be vastly more interconnected than expected beforehand; a minimally connected proposal is therefore almost surely wrong \emph{as stated}, however good its core idea. I offer that argument as a conjecture with an image rather than a theorem --- no measure on theory-space is specified, and I will not pretend one is --- but the image has teeth, and the axis it motivates can be checked against the record independently of it.

String theory is the live application, and I want to be exact about what the axis does and does not say there. The arguable core idea --- promoting some concept of worldsheet to relevance --- is quite clever. But the theory realizes the idea by postulating strings as a new primitive, which is precisely the substance side of the axis, and hence the diagnosis: the proposal as stated is almost surely not what the universe is doing, while the core idea, being good, probably retains shades of similarity (§\ref{sec:shades}) to whatever the universe is actually doing. What I would have recommended instead is keeping the worldsheet idea but evaluating it as an emergent projection from something deeper --- a reasoning move --- rather than dropping it into the system directly. The axis, note, does not score outcomes by pedigree or difficulty; it scores the \emph{kind} of move, and it makes a falsifiable bet about which kinds age well.

The axis has ancestors, and naming them is how its delta gets measured. The nearest is Einstein's own 1919 distinction between principle theories and constructive theories \cite{einstein1919times} --- thermodynamics and relativity as theories of principle, kinetic theory as construction from posited constituents. The kinship is real, but the claims differ in force: Einstein's was a taxonomy of forms; the present axis is a \emph{diagnostic} --- substance-addition as a predictor of failure --- which the 1919 taxonomy never asserted and which the constructive successes (kinetic theory itself) show must be probabilistic rather than exceptionless. The second neighbor is the distinction between progressive and degenerating research programmes \cite{lakatos1978methodology}, which tracks a programme's trajectory over time by whether its adjustments predict novel facts; the present axis is instead read off a single proposal's structure at birth, before any trajectory exists --- a prior where the programmes machinery is a posterior. Between the taxonomy that does not predict and the tracker that must wait, an axis that bets early and states what would refute it has, I think, its own honest work to do.

\section{Kinds of revolution: the ontological and the methodological}\label{sec:kinds}

The standard chronology of physics is a chronology of unifications, and I think its implicit goal is to categorize Newton and Maxwell the same way. As a heuristic for \emph{outcomes} that is solid enough; as an account of \emph{kinds} it misinterprets the record. When Faraday came out with his experiments and Maxwell went to write them up as mathematics, the task itself was of the shape ``carry the surprises unveiled by the experiments to axioms'' --- fundamentally important, and additive. What Newton did was of a different shape and different stakes: ``perform a titanic-level unscrambling in our own priors as humans, which has been stuck at an impasse since the Stone Age'' --- much more subtractive by weight than it was ever additive. To give the kinds names: Maxwell's is a methodological-conceptual revolution; Newton's is a categorically \emph{ontological} one. The distinction matters now because contemporary quantum foundations has been issued exactly the Newton-type assignment --- the call to unscramble within our own fundamental conception of causation and inference \cite{schmid2020unscrambling} --- and mistaking it for a Maxwell-type task is a way of guaranteeing it never gets done. One positioning paragraph against the obvious neighbor: the kinds distinction cross-cuts, rather than restates, the normal/revolutionary structure of \cite{kuhn1962structure} --- Maxwell's achievement was fully revolutionary in Kuhn's sense while methodological in mine, so the present distinction sorts \emph{among} revolutions by the character of the central move, where paradigm talk sorts revolutions from puzzle-solving; I use the word paradigm throughout in Kuhn's debt and with that difference flagged.

So that the kinds do not collapse into a headcount, a criterion independent of authorship: a revolution is \emph{ontological} exactly when its central move re-identifies what the theory's basic quantities are about --- an unscrambling of the referents in our own priors --- rather than extending, formalizing, or systematizing a referent already in place. By that criterion Newton and Einstein\footnote{Another interesting note I want to call out is that with particularly profound conceptual shifts, in general they tend to be characterizable as promoting to the very primitive of the ontological apparatus something which in the previous framework was a deduced identity. The canonical instance, perhaps, is Einstein's equivalence principle rendering the local character of free fall as inertial transforming a deduction from gravitational force to the root intrinsic property of space around which the new theory of gravity inferentially assembles. \par
In my own work on Aethic reasoning, once I instated the conceptual shift that is the relativity of determinacy \cite{AethusSemantics, Aethus} by means of the extrusion principle \cite{Aethus}, I then inferred upon completing the postulates that the Quantum Nexus and Powerset Invalidation Theorem must be in effect for consistency with quantum wave mechanics and the Born rule \cite{Aethus}, which in turn deduced the quantitative wave-particle duality relations and no-signaling theorem prior to the quantum wavefunction rather than after it \cite{AethusQuant, Aethus}.} classify as ontological and Maxwell as methodological on the moves themselves, with no one counted. My base understanding of the surrounding sociology is Kuhnian \cite{kuhn1962structure}: the individual and the collective are both absolutely indispensable to the flow of progress --- individuals for sparking paradigm shifts, the collective for the normal science between them --- and the great-man-versus-collective debate borders on na\"ive in insisting we pick one; the body depends on both the heart and the spine, and removing either kills instantly. Any appeal to the historical record of ``great individuals'' also carries an obligation stated plainly: that record is severely compromised by the systematic exclusion of women, people of color, and other marginalized groups from education and scientific institutions --- an exclusion that speaks only to the cruelty of the structures enforcing it and says nothing whatsoever about the capacities of those it targeted, the conflation of denied access with diminished ability being among the most damaging errors in the history of ideas. The irony is particularly bitter\footnote{This disconnect is one of those rare things that actually brings me physical rage: the juxtaposition of denying individuals gratitude because of race or gender with then targeting those same people for reeling under that discrimination --- for struggling to reproduce the output stats of the white men those same institutions babied.} when you also consider how the malice behind historical systemic discrimination muzzles the very class of ontology-capable individuals we should be prizing --- while the very system doing the muzzling insists that individuals are special.

Where I differ from the base Kuhnian account is one attribution. The record then suggests a demographic regularity, stated as the conjecture it is rather than as a historical result: that the ontological move has, historically, been formulated by a single mind. The claim rests on four individuations a specialist historian is entitled to contest before granting it---what individuates an ``ontological move''; why Newton and not Newton with Huygens, Hooke, and Leibniz; why Einstein and not Einstein with Lorentz, Poincar\'e, and Grossmann; and whether ``single mind'' is partly manufactured by naming conventions and retrospective attribution---and until those are settled on a corpus not selected by outcome, the regularity is a conjecture the cases illustrate. It neither ranks the individual above the collective nor romanticizes anything; it identifies the one feature of progress that appears individually necessary while everything else is collectively performed. Quantum mechanics is the test case, and the criterion lets it fall where it falls: collectively built, magnificently systematized --- the Dirac equation easily as elegant as special relativity --- and, on the referent question, still open in the precise sense that no candidate re-identification has been collectively ratified (Everettians and Bohmians claim the move completed, without commanding ratification), while the causal-inferential program \cite{schmid2020unscrambling} treats the unscrambling of causation and inference that would constitute quantum theory's ontological move as the outstanding task, not a completed collective achievement. The regularity thus survives without fiat: the collective built everything about quantum mechanics except the one move the criterion tracks, and no performed candidate for that move has been collectively ratified.

On the inevitability thesis \cite{hacking1999social} I will be as delicate as I can while still saying it: I think special relativity, and general relativity above all, would have been quite a long shot without their author --- the ``thin film'' of ontology is, in my experience, overestimated in prospect and underestimated in retrospect. Everything may be in the air empirically, as the measurement problem has been since at least the 1940s; but Poincar\'e was brilliant and ontology was not his thing, and if the collective's access to tiny ontological perturbations were what it is assumed to be, some anthropic reasoner would have reached the Accordance Principle \cite{NexicFoundation} long ago. The collective finds such perturbations far more inaccessible than is typically assumed, and living amid the further revolutions none of us have achieved is, for the inevitability thesis, like living the refutation of one's thesis without noticing it. The section compresses to one principle, stated in the source development and adopted here as this paper's motto for the ontological kind: \textbf{\emph{scientific progress is only ever as good as its ontological content}}.

There is a further asymmetry worth naming, because it explains why the ontological kind is so rare outside physics. Distinguish a concept's \emph{intrinsic} advancedness from its \emph{perceived} advancedness --- the latter read off our own paradigmatic orientation, the former off something closer to inferential fruitfulness. My proposal is that philosophy as a discipline carries an abnormally high ratio of perceived to intrinsic advancedness, and physics the lowest, with the human-nuance-inviting disciplines grading between. The reason is that philosophy is perpetually stuck in a paradigm of terminology where trivial statements are arbitrarily hard to even state and standing unifications go unrecognized --- a diagnosis with a natural ally in the analysis of merely verbal disputes \cite{chalmers2011verbal} --- a state I would compare directly to physics before Newton, or, at the frontier, to quantum field theory today, where one senses a clean partitioning in the universe's own contributions while our drawn borders intersect them so chaotically that it is impossible to parse how much of what we write is ontological signal and how much is the noise of human convention. The difference between science and philosophy is then that each new scientific revolution is low-hanging fruit compared to a philosophical revolution of comparable intrinsic advancedness: the scientific case has an ontological core underneath, which provides just enough leverage that a Newton-caliber mind alone can access it. The compounding difficulty as one moves away from convention-impartial ontology is, I will suggest in §\ref{sec:shades}, not incidental --- it is the lexicon itself doing the scrambling.

\section{Two stages of a breakthrough: charming and structural}\label{sec:stages}

\begin{criterion}{The two-stage model}
Inference-driven breakthroughs tend to unfold in two stages: a \emph{charming stage}, in which the researcher has intuited a basic principle of the universe but still views it from within a subtly anthropocentric vantage; and a culminating \emph{structural stage}, from which the charming stage reveals itself as having been charmingly naive about the tightness with which the universe actually does things.
\end{criterion}

Where the universe does things like an all-business program director --- every move a symphony in ontological graph-connective contribution --- a human first gaining a partial copy of the memo tends to situate the plan in terms of what is familiar, and that is where the retrospective naivety comes from. Two historical cases clued me into the phenomenon. Feynman's ``Plenty of Room at the Bottom'' lecture \cite{feynman1960room} initiates squarely in the charming stage: the foundational idea of nanotechnology intuited --- retrospectively discussed as prophetic --- while the concrete proposal of iterative hands proved unnecessary to the prizes themselves, the first won unexpectedly quickly by classically available means, the second later by a more streamlined method matching the idea while stripping the flanks. And Einstein's road to general relativity reads as the model's cleanest instance: the equivalence principle as the charming stage --- the principle intuited, still situated in elevators and observers --- and the transition to the structural stage arriving with general covariance, where the familiar scaffolding fell away and the constraint stood alone.

\section{Shades of similarity, and why deep unknowns hide}\label{sec:shades}

\begin{criterion}{Shades of similarity}
The deepest unknowns tend to have shades of similarity to all things known. It is hard to discover them not because they are new, but because they resemble conflicting sides of existing debates.
\end{criterion}

The mechanism is camouflage. A deep unknown that resembled nothing would at least register as an anomaly; one that resembles \emph{both} sides of a standing debate gets claimed by each side as a flavor of what it already holds and dismissed by each side as a flavor of what it already rejects --- and so it hides in plain sight, misfiled rather than missed. The thesis makes a standing prediction about reception that I state here as a falsifiable expectation for my own framework: readers will systematically classify it as a variant of whichever position they already occupy, and the misclassifications will be confident, articulate, and mutually inconsistent. Stated bare, that prediction doubles as armor --- any criticism files as confirmation, the conventionalist stratagem this paper's own citations condemn --- so here is the exit condition that immunizes the immunization. A \emph{correct} classification is one that locates the framework by its own stated deltas: each companion paper carries an explicit statement of where it diverges from its nearest neighbors, and a reception that names those deltas --- agrees or disagrees with them, but names them --- scores as correct. The prediction is scored on aggregate published reception tallied against those delta statements, and it \emph{fails} if the modal published response locates the deltas accurately; camouflage, on this scoring, is a measurable property of a literature's response, not a universal escape hatch.

I now want to argue that this camouflage operates at a level deeper than concepts: it is built into the words. Take a tree model of cognition --- cognitive branches, each with its own set of intuitions, one branch having happened to write the public lexicon. (The documented instance is the allistic/autistic case, where the diagnostic binary inadvertently classifies people as ``non-allistic'' for lacking one branch's traits, on the direct analogy of the pre-Darwinian human/animal dichotomy against the post-Darwinian tree; but the argument runs on branches generally, and nothing below depends on the clinical vocabulary.) Consider the standard characterization of mathematics as ``logical'' --- right and wrong answers --- against the humanities as ``nuanced.'' Anyone learning the most introductory layer of a topic tailored to a different branch's intuitive landscape reports the same characterization: kindergarten literature has clear rules --- either the letter is written this way or it is not --- and sits at an isomorphic introductory level to twelfth-grade mathematics. Comparing twelfth-grade mathematics to twelfth-grade literature is therefore the wrong reference class; the felt contrast measures the general population's higher intuitive capacity for literature, not the topics. Had the branch with the flipped capacities written the linguistic rules, mathematics would be described as highly nuanced and flexible --- as it already is by the world's great mathematicians --- and Shakespearean literature as a rigid system of writing down letters and sounding them out. Symmetry under re-choice of reference branch is the sign of a good universalization, and this vocabulary has none.

Call the resulting phenomenon \emph{linguistic masking}: the use of dominant-branch-perspectival vocabulary to describe one's own internal cognitive phenomenology. The phenomenon is proposed here as a general one, and it is stated as a hypothesis with its test attached rather than as an observation that establishes itself: give blinded readers semantically novel theses whose surface language closely resembles established positions, with the delta explicitly present in the text, and measure whether they systematically assimilate the thesis to the nearest familiar category anyway. A single reader's misparse, human or machine, is an instance generating the hypothesis; the hypothesis is confirmed or not by that experiment, and nothing below leans on its truth. The public cognitive lexicon is not a neutral medium but a branch-specific dialect that other branches must translate themselves into, often by silently redefining the available terms while preserving their public surface --- producing semantic drift the speakers do not notice, or leaving labels standing with subtleties behind them that no other branch is aware were arrived at. The person who multiplies eight-digit numbers by intuitive nuance will still call mathematics ``rigid and logical,'' mirroring where the dominant branch applies the word rather than reporting their own calibration --- and the labor is asymmetric and continuous, extracted by the standing lexicon whether or not anyone is presently misreading you.

The cleanest exhibit on record is Grothendieck's famous dislike of examples \cite{jackson2004comme, grothendieck1986recoltes} --- with the reading that follows marked plainly as my conjecture about his interior, not as biography. From within his historically rare cognitive branch there are plenty of things semantically consistent with examples --- picture an order-five abstraction grasped by how three order-four objects fit together in various ways: each snapshot shows a facet of a deeper thing in a targeted simplified context, which \emph{is} the semantics of an example --- but only Grothendieck could recognize such an object as one, since nobody else gets past a single order-three object in a frame of consciousness. What everyone else definitionally categorizes as ``examples'' is the same construction run over order-two objects, and \emph{that} category of instances --- not the semantic concept --- is what Grothendieck reports disliking. The mismatch then locks from both directions: he assigns his own semantic character to the dominant branch's categorized instances, they read his sentence under their semantics, his biographers explain an eccentricity he never had, and the concept he actually valued --- his own branch's example --- likely never gets written down at all, occupying as it does the base of his cognition beneath everything he names. Deep unknowns hide behind conceptual resemblance; deep \emph{minds} hide behind lexical resemblance; and the second mechanism feeds the first, since the unifications the lexicon scrambles are exactly the ones §\ref{sec:kinds} says philosophy fails to recognize.

The move that generates such reframes deserves its own name, because it is not local to cognition: call it \emph{localizing the flex to the environment}. Confronted with the fact that apes do not ask questions \cite{jordania2006who, premack1983mind}, do not conclude a missing faculty; instead import full human categorical cognition into the ape unchanged, and ask what their contextual environment would have to look like to reproduce the no-questions behavior for consistency --- whereupon the answer arrives as a boundary-prediction difficulty compounded by language funneled through blinders, and an ape asking a question is priced correctly: as historically anomalous as Sequoyah, illiterate, deriving the Cherokee syllabary by inference alone. This is, in retrospect, exactly the move that gave rise to Einstein's equivalence principle --- one of the highest-caliber scientific ideas in known history, and methodologically informative for that reason: hold Earth's phenomenological setting constant, import the accelerating-laboratory empiricism fixed, and let Earth's structural-contextual environment vary for consistency; the result is that spacetime must be curved here. The reframe and the principle are one method --- keep the interior constant, flex the context --- and it is the method by which a dominant branch's deficit-readings of other minds get replaced by structure.

One more unification, because it converts the whole discussion into a prediction problem. Communicating across a cognition branch and predicting the pinpoint structures of a future society are, I claim, the same difficulty rather than categorically different ones: design a Turing-style test in which an objective actor must distinguish a communication across a paradigm boundary from a one-way communication aimed at an unknown future society, and the actor cannot distinguish them on categorical grounds --- each could be labeled as the other while remaining consistent. So whoever wishes to price the true difficulty of crossing any such boundary, without falling back on heuristics, should simply ask how difficult it would be to predict the pinpoint nuances of the societal structures thirty years from now. The camouflage thesis, the lexicon thesis, and the boundary-prediction unification are one phenomenon at three depths: the unknown resembles the known, the words belong to one branch, and the far side of any deep boundary is exactly as unforecastable as the future.

The preceding section explains why deep unknowns hide. This one draws the consequence for evaluation, and it inverts a heuristic evaluators use daily: shades of similarity make \emph{short} gaps disproportionately hard to close. A position crowded by near neighbors reads as occupied from every direction --- the density of the neighborhood is mistaken for coverage of the point --- so the closer the standing debate presses around an unoccupied bullseye, the more certainly each camp files the vacancy as territory it already holds, and the harder it becomes for anyone to see that the decisive spot is empty. The firewall debate of §\ref{sec:claim} is the worked instance, and its standoff is external history: for some eight decades the discovery/justification argument ran between a camp that discarded the context and a camp that hunted rules for it, while the split position --- no rules, full evidential retention --- sat between them resembling both and claimed by neither camp; a middle that near is precisely what this section predicts goes unoccupied longest.\footnote{Unoccupied by the camps, though not unstated: Hoyningen-Huene, cataloguing the distinction's versions, located its defensible core in the difference between the descriptive and the normative perspectives on science \cite{hoyningen1987context, hoyningen2006context}, which admits the facts of discovery to description and reserves appraisal for the normative perspective. That discharges the claim that the position went unstated, by the rule stated below; which of \S\ref{sec:retrodiction}'s bins the instance then falls in turns on how the camps engaged that statement, which is not assessed here.} Two disciplines follow. For evaluation: when a contribution's deltas read as small against a dense neighborhood, the evidence of its difficulty is not the size of the textual delta but the duration of the vacancy under that density --- similarity is a fact about the terrain's crowding, and down-weighting novelty by resemblance systematically penalizes exactly the contributions this section predicts are hardest to make. The correction is not a sign-flip: ``close means low significance'' is the fallacy, and ``close means high significance'' would be its equally naive mirror --- worse than naive, in fact, since a proximity-scoring rule would attempt to axiomatize a leg of inductive evaluation, exactly the move §\ref{sec:art} argues cannot be made. The asymmetry is worth one more sentence, because it locates the line exactly: a positive comparative is monotone --- affirm ``closer is harder'' as a principle and you have asserted ``farther is easier'' in the same breath, a commitment nobody holds --- while the negative form, \emph{do not assume closer is easier}, asserts no ordering and denies two: it calls false ``closer is universally easier'' and calls false ``farther is universally easier'' alike --- the two universals the fallacy and its mirror respectively require --- leaving the terrain order-free, structure removed and none installed, which is the only shape of guidance an art admits. The two condemned universals even co-sign a shared assumption --- that difficulty is a monotone function of proximity at all --- and the tip is the cut along exactly that co-signature boundary: the corollary's own machinery, run one level down on its own evaluation rule. Negatives of that species sit safely on the tips side of §\ref{sec:art}'s line that heuristics are not axioms, the register in which writing keeps Vonnegut's eight rules and remains an art. The correction is an added \emph{column}: the full price of a contribution is the delta statement together with a vacancy report --- duration and density, the latter obliged to name the currency it is counted in, since closeness measured in shared formal apparatus can be spurious where the missing piece is ontological (\S\ref{sec:retrodiction}) --- with one discriminator carrying the weight: an \emph{unstated} vacancy is what camouflage predicts, while a \emph{stated-and-rejected} one leaves tracks and counts against the closer --- refined at \S\ref{sec:retrodiction}, where a first run forces a third bin, \emph{stated-but-unengaged}, which is masking rather than rejection, and narrows the second to rejections that engaged the version later accepted. A claim that a position went unstated is written to be defeated by exactly such a track: a critic who produces a prior statement of the position has not merely scored a point but discharged the claim, and the corollary tells them so in advance. And for honesty, the conditional runs one way only: proximity-camouflage explains, \emph{given} a proposal's correctness, why its gap stayed open and why its neighbors miss something decisive for a subset of applications; it is not itself evidence of correctness, and nothing in this corollary upgrades resemblance into support --- the scoring rule above remains the only cash this thesis pays.

The corollary also has a dynamic form, and the dynamics are worse than the statics. A fierce two-sided debate is not a neutral search over its question: each camp's advocacy promotes everything the camps share --- their shared-right and their shared-wrong alike --- because the fight concentrates all critical attention on the contested axis while the common background rides along co-signed by both sides at once, gaining apparent solidity with every round precisely because no round examines it. Call this the \emph{co-signature loop}. Closing the gap then requires something no amount of further fighting supplies: cutting the possibility space exactly along the boundary between what the camps share rightly and what they share wrongly --- keep the first, discard the second --- and that cut is excruciating from within the paradigm, first because shared assumptions are invisible as commitments (nothing in the debate marks them as claims at all), and second because the loop has been compounding their credibility for decades. The firewall instance now reads one level deeper: what the two camps co-signed was the coupling of the discovery context's evidential status to its normative status --- take discovery seriously and seek rules for it, or refuse rules and discard the records --- and the split position is precisely the cut along the co-signature boundary, keeping each camp's right half while discarding the assumption both were fighting on top of. Stated as an image rather than a theorem, in the manner of §\ref{sec:axis}'s graph conjecture: a debate is a one-dimensional contested axis through a very high-dimensional possibility space; scrutiny flows along the axis; the orthogonal complement --- where the shared-wrong lives --- receives co-signature instead of scrutiny; and the cut that closes the gap is a hyperplane the debate's own gradient never points at. Which is why prolonged fierceness and prolonged vacancy are found together.

The corollary complex has one standing relative that deserves its name here: the problem of unconceived alternatives \cite{stanford2006exceeding}. Stanford's historical case series establishes that working scientists systematically fail to conceive of alternatives that later inquiry finds compelling, and deploys the fact as a new induction against scientific realism. The relation is a division of labor with the delta stated at both ends: his record establishes \emph{that} alternatives go unconceived; the corollary and the loop propose \emph{which} and \emph{why} --- the near ones, because density reads as coverage, and compounded, because fierce debate credentials the co-signed background --- while the vacancy column converts the diagnosis into an evaluation practice rather than an argument about realism, on which nothing here takes a side. The engagement also yields a check that someone else can run: if the mechanism is right, Stanford's unconceived alternatives should cluster in the neighborhoods their eras' fiercest debates crowded most densely, and his own published cases are the natural test bed --- a retrodiction stated here in advance of anyone running it.

\subsection{A first run of the retrodiction}\label{sec:retrodiction}
The check just stated was run once, in the session that installed it, and is reported here with its results and its corrections --- two of which fall against the corollary rather than for it.

The two accounts are complementary rather than rival, and that is what makes a check possible at all. Stanford's own proposed mechanism is cognitive-general: he suggests that the failure to consider relevant alternatives in theoretical contexts may be a general property of human cognition \cite{stanford2006exceeding}. The corollary proposes a structural amplifier on top of whatever that baseline rate is --- and the two differ about \emph{location}. A purely cognitive-general account predicts no particular relation between where a vacancy sits and how its era's debate was structured; the co-signature loop predicts the vacancy sits in the co-signed complement rather than anywhere on the contested axis.

Protocol, with its weakness stated first: for each case, identify the era's fiercest debate in the domain, identify the alternative later communities accepted, and code whether that alternative required negating an assumption both camps held --- or instead occupied an open position on the contested axis, which counts as a miss. The weakness is that the cases were selected and coded by the corollary's own proponents; this is exactly the confirmation structure this paper's protocol flags on anyone else, and it is flagged here.

Stanford's core series --- Darwin's pangenesis, Galton's stirp theory, Weismann's germ-plasm \cite{stanford2006pangenesis, stanford2006galton, stanford2005weismann} --- codes as a fit. The fiercest contemporaneous axis in that domain was the inheritance of acquired characters: whether the soma writes to the germ line. What all three theories co-signed across that divide is that heredity and development are one problem solved by a material representing the organism's parts, development being the parceling out of that representative material --- gemmules thrown off by the parts, stirp elements standing for them, determinants distributed differentially as cells specialize. The alternative later communities accepted negates the co-signature rather than either camp's position: heredity as combinatorial rules over discrete factors that segregate, and differentiation as differential \emph{expression} of a complement every cell retains rather than differential \emph{distribution} of a complement every cell spends. That negation arrived in two stages separated by half a century --- segregation with Mendel's rediscovery in 1900, the retained-complement picture only with mid-twentieth-century work on differentiation --- and the coding above credits the co-signature's defeat to the pair, not to either alone. No further answer about whether the soma writes to the germ line --- no position on the contested axis --- is what closed the gap. One qualification belongs with that coding, and it cuts: the axis was fiercely contested mainly in the 1880s and 1890s, Weismann against the neo-Lamarckians, whereas at the Darwin end of the window the shared assumption sat nearer consensus than contest. The series therefore supports the statics --- an unexamined shared background --- across its whole length, and supports the dynamics specifically, where a live fight credentials that background, only in its later window.

Four further cases, drawn from the standard canon rather than from Stanford's own case studies --- of these, the ether and caloric are named among his book's background examples, while light and drift are this paper's additions --- code as follows. Light: the wave--corpuscle debate co-signed both an exclusive disjunction and the demand for a mechanical substrate, and the accepted alternative denied the disjunction. The electrodynamics of moving bodies: the contending ether models co-signed absolute simultaneity, and the accepted alternative denied it. This case is usually told as the proximity claim's most famous instance --- the nearest neighbours, holding the transformations already, being precisely who did not close it --- and the telling needs a correction that cuts against this section's own convenience. The standard historiography treats the pre-1905 work on the dynamics of the electron as supplying most of the structure of special relativity, with the ontological completion as the piece still missing \cite{pais1982subtle, holton1988thematic}; that framing prices the ontology as one more brick for the wall. The corpus's own treatment rejects the pricing \cite{Outliers}: the ontological substrate is not a brick but the tree the mathematical pieces hang on --- remove it and what is left is not a nearly finished theory but broken glass on the floor --- and that substrate is itself the long sequential chain rather than the short step left over. Two consequences follow for the machinery of this section, both restrictive. Apparent proximity counted in shared formal apparatus systematically overstates closeness wherever the vacancy is ontological, so a vacancy report must declare the currency its density is counted in. And the impression that such a result was ``in the air'' is partly manufactured by its own arrival: a field can circle a problem for a century, and an early landing then makes the whole approach look as though it had been nearly complete all along --- with the possession of every loose mathematical piece being at once the reason the thing looks solvable and the reason it stays unsolved. Continental drift: fixism and drift co-signed a static ocean floor, and the accepted alternative moved the floor. Heat codes as a partial fit only, and is reported as such: the kinetic camp did win its axis, and the co-signed background --- heat as a classical-mechanical property throughout --- failed later and elsewhere.

One finding arrived unbidden, on a different thesis. Coded on \S\ref{sec:axis}, Stanford's series sorts cleanly: each failed theory adds \emph{substance} --- gemmules, stirp elements, determinants --- installed to keep standing reasoning about representative inheritance intact, while the accepted alternative adds \emph{reasoning}, its discrete factors entailed by the combinatorial rules the ratios demanded rather than installed to rescue anything. That is the axis running correctly on a case series someone else selected for an unrelated purpose, which is the only kind of external test that costs its author nothing to fail.

Four corrections are forced, against material installed earlier in this section and against the run itself. First, the vacancy discriminator's two bins are insufficient. Mendel's paper stood in print from 1866 and functioned as unconceived until 1900: a \emph{stated-but-unengaged} vacancy, which is masking rather than rejection, and which the two-bin rule would have miscounted as evidence against the closer. Drift forces a further split: it was stated and rejected for a real reason --- no mechanism --- and the vacancy persisted anyway for the modified version that later won, so a rejection can be correct on the stated form while the gap stays open. The rule that a stated-and-rejected vacancy counts against the closer is accordingly weakened to this: it counts against only where the rejection engaged the version later accepted. Second, the run does not test the retrodiction as stated. That prediction was distributional --- vacancies \emph{cluster} where debates crowded densest --- and clustering requires a contrast class this run never assembled: dense debates that produced no vacancy, and vacancies arising where no dense debate stood. What was actually tested is the conditional question, given a vacancy, whether it sat in the co-signed complement. That is worth knowing and it is a different claim; the distributional version remains untested, and \S\ref{sec:pricing}'s statement of it should be read accordingly. Third, every case examined is a famous filled vacancy, which is selection on the outcome, and the run returned no misses at all --- a result to be read as evidence about the coder before it is read as evidence about the world. Two candidate misses were not tested and belong in any independent run: phlogiston to oxygen, where the accepted alternative arguably occupied the contested axis rather than negating it, the co-signed premise of a transferred principle surviving the reversal of its direction; and miasma to germ theory, where the contagionist position stood on the axis throughout. Fourth, and without softening: one self-coded run over partly familiar cases is not a result. It is a pilot.

What would make it a result is specified here in advance, so that the specification is dated rather than fitted. Fix the case list to Stanford's three published case studies and add none. Have the debate-density and co-signature coding done by someone with no stake in the corollary, working from primary sources rather than from this description. Count as a miss any case where the later-accepted alternative occupies a position on the contested axis instead of negating a co-signed assumption. The corollary predicts fits in most; a cognitive-general account alone predicts no such concentration. Any independent run should also engage the critical literature on this same series, which has been re-analyzed with the argument that biologists avoid this inferential pattern where they can and hedge where they cannot \cite{novick2020presume} --- a challenge to the case study's framing that bears directly on how every case above should be coded.

\section{Induction as an art form}\label{sec:art}

\begin{criterion}{The art thesis}
Inductive reasoning is an art form, while deductive reasoning is part of the artwork. And: inductive reasoning is the art of editing your own faulty assumptions from new data.
\end{criterion}
\noindent The thesis is stated at its defensible strength, which is conditional and not categorical. What the negative argument establishes is that current deep discovery appears to require open-ended representational revision that no fixed deductive or inductive algorithm captures; the word ``art'' names that feature, and does not prove its irreducibility. Machine discovery, Bayesian experimental design, automated conjecture generation, program synthesis, and evolutionary search all press on a categorical reading, and none of them presently reproduces the reframing this paper cares about; the exit condition below---revise the thesis if a machine independently performs the reframing---is the discipline that keeps the claim conditional now rather than after the fact.

Because so much of the medium of science is nomological, deductive reasoning becomes perhaps its central algorithmic feature --- the conversion of first principles into applications, utterly central to scientific output at least as far back as Newton. But that is on the completed-artwork side, and tells you nothing about how to produce the artwork. Good writing is not good because it follows a set of rules, and good visual art is not good because it follows a set of rules; the best available guidance for inductive practice is therefore of the kind of Vonnegut's eight rules for writing fiction \cite{vonnegut1999bagombo} --- decentralized tips on how flavors pop up across good practice, operating in a heavily flexible medium, and famously issued alongside the permission of great writers to break every one of them.

The argument for the thesis, rather than the analogy, is a regress. Suppose one declared deductive first principles for good inductive reasoning. Induction's entire office --- per the second box --- is the dethroning of currently faulty assumptions; axiomatizing the practice would smuggle the era's assumptions into the axioms themselves, defeating the purpose the practice exists for. The regress reaches first principles that carry the era's substantive assumptions; a fixed procedure need not carry them, since its rules can direct the replacement of a hypothesis the evidence defeats, staying fixed while its commitments change, and the regress does not exclude such a procedure. The way an assumption gets dethroned is flexibility and visibility into loopholes, which is why the working posture is the black-hatted outlaw who games vulnerabilities, not the sheriff who follows every rule --- and why, when I see somebody invent the axioms of inductive reasoning, what I see is somebody who has spent a lifetime inside the finished artwork and thinks that experience transfers to the artwork-making. The thesis owes an answer to its strongest opponent, and the opponent has a name. Herbert Simon spent a career arguing that scientific discovery has a logic \cite{simon1973logic}, and built, with colleagues, programs that rediscovered Kepler's third law from data \cite{langley1987scientific}; contemporary machine-assisted conjecture generation renews the pressure --- and this very paper documents an AI-assisted process while asserting there are no rules of discovery. The resolution is the line the Vonnegut framing already draws: \emph{heuristics are not axioms}. The regress argument targets axiomatization --- fixed first principles that would freeze the current assumptions into the method --- and Simon's own machinery is on the near side of that line: heuristic search under human-prepared representations, tips operating in a flexible medium at scale, artwork-side pattern extraction that presupposes rather than performs the reframing this paper calls the making. On the same line, the corpus's own drafting collaboration classifies cleanly: the machine amplified --- searched, formalized, verified, pressed objections --- while every reframing ruling ran through the author's taste, and no rule set generated the rulings; the outlaw kept the wheel, the sheriff got a faster horse. Should a machine one day perform the reframing itself, unprompted, the thesis would owe revision --- I state that as the thesis's own exit condition rather than defend against it in advance. What the archive shows is accordingly the narrower claim: reframing judgments that were not articulated as rules, and that no procedure yet specified produces; the art thesis characterizes discovery by them, and is not a proof that no procedure could produce them.

Note carefully what the thesis does \emph{not} say: it places no limit on rigor after the making. The firewall position of §\ref{sec:claim} is exactly this division of labor --- no rules of discovery, exacting rules of checking --- and the two postures work different shifts on the same job.

The thesis also settles a standing misreading. ``Inductive reasoning carries special cases to general cases'' is correct as a retrospective and misinformative as a procedure --- and that procedural misinformation is exactly where a familiar category error breeds: the resistance of inductive practice to axiomatization gets read as an open problem, when it is simply the categorical nature of an art form. This is the calibration in which I engage the classic confirmation paradoxes \cite{hempel1945studies, goodman1955fact}. Those puzzles arose within and against the formal-confirmation program --- Hempel's ravens as an internal puzzle of confirmation theory, Goodman's grue against its syntactic core --- and the positive conclusion their best author reached --- projection settled by entrenched practice rather than by logic --- already concedes much of the negative point; my target is the surrounding reception, the idea that grue creates a foundational crisis in how to do inductive reasoning, as if axiomatic inductive reasoning were a live option rather than the thing the regress argument above rules out. In actual practice it is a question of taste --- exactly as in art --- whether a perturbation to a concept is to be entertained; no heuristic for when to make those leaps would be honest, because the freedom to work the shape of what is needed by feel is what categorically makes success possible at all.

Last, the conjecture that says why the medium is as messy as the thesis requires --- offered, per this paper's discipline, as a conjecture with an image. Take any deep standing model and let its complexity divide into proportion $\alpha$ human bias and proportion $1 - \alpha$ ontological nomology. Deduction operates entirely in the $1-\alpha$ subspace --- all nomology-to-application carrying happens there --- and yet, measuring dimensional complexity, the universe does things simply relative to the accumulation of human biases and misinterpretations over the millennia: $1-\alpha$ is measure zero with respect to $\alpha$, as if comparing the fractal dimension of the directional field of Einsteinian gravity against that of a brain. No measure is specified and I will not pretend otherwise; but if anything like the image holds, then the ability for inductive reasoning is the ability to parse the $\alpha$-measure subspace and compress it into something transferable to the $1-\alpha$ part --- work that must be done scrappily, by feel, by artists by trade, because knowing what to expect ahead of time is exactly the luxury that $\alpha$'s existence denies.

A corollary on mathematical beauty closes the section, because the art thesis is often mistaken for the Platonic one and the difference is exact. Mathematical beauty is a subjective concept --- two people rate the same concepts differently --- and unaided human guessing over the space of \emph{arbitrary formal statements} succeeds at an infinitesimal hit rate that fails exponentially faster under compounding criteria; the reference class matters, since trained conjecture over structured domains does far better --- Ramanujan is the standing existence proof --- and the argument below concerns the former class, where the beauty signal has to do its work. What beauty supplies is therefore not truth but \emph{non-mediocrity}: since truth necessarily fails probabilistic mediocrity inside standard human intuitive capacities, only what stands out at an unusual quantile is even eligible, and the startling character that subjectively marks a formalism as beautiful is precisely what clears that hurdle. Beauty and truth are thus strongly correlated without either causing the other: beauty buys a candidate its \emph{chance}, generally still far from likely. This also positions the corollary beside its near-ally: the argument that beauty has led physics astray \cite{hossenfelder2018lost} is, on the present account, half right --- beauty never was a truth-maker, and treating eligibility as likelihood is exactly the error; what the corollary preserves is beauty's legitimate office as a non-mediocrity filter, nothing more. The working practice this licenses is augmentation rather than divination. Concretely: needing a $y$ that meets three criteria while direct intuition supports only two, set $y = \sin(2\arctan x)$ for an intermediate $x$ fitted to the two --- whereupon the third criterion, boundedness, arrives free from the identity web itself ($\sin(2\arctan x) = 2x/(1+x^2)$, confined to $[-1,1]$ by construction) --- the identity web of trigonometry, whose internal structure outguesses any human; the discipline's own elegance, not the practitioner's eye, is what reaches the final gap.

The thesis suggests a conjecture in the sociology of evaluation, worth stating with the same one-way discipline as everything else here. A metric that could \emph{proactively} and reliably identify frontier inductive capacity --- score the genius before the output --- would, I conjecture, have to span the space of moves it claims to score, a generator in disguise whose existence would constitute exactly the rules of discovery this essay denies; but forecasting who is more likely to do significant work is not producing that work, and a forecast could rest on imperfect indicators without reconstructing anyone's future reasoning, so the step is a conjecture about proxies and not a consequence of the art thesis. The direction matters: verifying a \emph{delivered} result is often cheap, and that asymmetry is the whole economy of proof, but scoring an \emph{undelivered} one requires modeling the prover, and no such model is on offer --- our best statistical evaluators, whatever else they do, are not emitting theories of everything. The corpus's own outlier-productivity study sharpens this asymmetry rather than softening it \cite{Outliers}: capacity of the relevant kind is measurable \emph{retrospectively}, from achieved sequences and the step counts they required, and the measurement returns extreme values --- which is exactly what makes the proactive case hopeless, since the retrospective score is computed from the very output whose existence a proactive score would have had to anticipate. So the fallibility of genius-metrics is, on the art thesis, to be expected rather than an embarrassment awaiting reform --- a conjecture about proxies, whose success for a given population is an empirical question --- and its signature failure mode follows: the rare uncredentialed person significantly outpacing the credentialed is \emph{expected}, because credentials are a retrospective proxy pressed into proactive service, and the proxy false-negatives precisely on the profiles whose making it cannot span --- the person-level analog of §\ref{sec:pricing}'s vacancy discount, a metric-shaped markdown landing on exactly the hard cases. The conjecture has kin it should name: signaling theory prices credentials as deliberately lossy proxies \cite{spence1973job}, the credential-society literature documents the proxy's social career \cite{collins1979credential}, and Goodhart's law describes proxy corruption under targeting \cite{goodhart1975problems} --- while Meehl's classic case for statistical over clinical prediction \cite{meehl1954clinical} sits in no conflict with any of this, its superiority results living in criterion-ready tasks where the output space is already spanned, which is exactly the condition the frontier lacks. The record this paper carries contains one candidate instance in the raw: the 2023 moderation decline preserved in the correspondence archive, asking after a conventional publication record --- the metric caught in the act. Whether it issued a \emph{false} negative is not this paper's to declare; by the standing conditional, that adjudication belongs to the external record the archive exists to enable, and the conjecture claims only the base rate: in any regime measured by proxies weaker than the thing itself, such cases must occur.

\subsection{The stages, annotated}\label{sec:stages_annotated}

I annotate each stage with the thesis it instantiates, and I begin much earlier than the framework, because the record does.

\emph{Preschool.} On a Windows XP machine I discovered, by moving to the root directory and descending a different pathway, that the file explorer reaches the control panel --- a location I had internalized as a distinct, closed-off domain of the start panel. The thought in the moment: two categorically distinct domains can be welded together beneath the surface, two vantages on a single underlying domain whose distinction lives in your priors rather than in the structure. The inner knowing that arrived with it --- \emph{the barrier between any two things is an illusion} --- is my first epiphany on record, and the reader will recognize it as the substance/reasoning axis in embryo: the profundity was a deleted boundary, not an added thing.

\emph{Kindergarten.} I imagined addition as preparing and projecting a number forward on the number line --- $2+3$ preparing to move forward from $2$ by $3$, the equals sign running the command --- and then dreamt up an alternative in which one changes not the operation but the \emph{running command}: the same preparation, a different mathematical object to run it, keeping the degrees of freedom intact while localizing the change to the qualitative unfolding of the run --- ideally projecting the result off the number line altogether, to the alphabet, or, given enough iterations, to something metaphysical. I had not yet learned subtraction existed; the record thus carries, at age five, the pattern that governs everything after --- alien methodological conclusions arrived at simply through lack of exposure to standard practice --- and an explicit prelude to the function and set-extension intuitions, arguably to the functor, since the synesthetic emphasis sat on the carried structure. \emph{Grade school through high school.} A fourth-grade shower thought: wanting $17^2$ from a memorized $16^2 = 256$ with no paper, I reasoned that adding sixteen makes sixteen sixteens into seventeen sixteens, and since seventeen sixteens is sixteen seventeens, adding seventeen makes seventeen seventeens --- $256 + 16 + 17 = 289$ --- the identity $a^2 - (a-1)^2 = a + (a-1)$ found as mental arithmetic. A seventh-grade night intuition, written down the next morning: a formula moving three numbers down a conveyor belt so each takes the next one's place --- normalize the first two to zero and one, match the rescaled gap to the next difference, translate --- which is linear-and-exponential interpolation generalized, invented without having heard of interpolation. Between a few hundred and perhaps a thousand such identities and consequences accumulated, some fundamental, some not; the fundamental theorem of calculus, for the record's second column, was never among them.

\section{Who can see past entrenchment: a testable leg}\label{sec:entrenchment}

Everything above treats entrenchment as the enemy and taste as the weapon; this short section converts that talk into a hypothesis. The question: is the classical, objectivist-deterministic mindset innate to humans, or taught in infancy by a society that runs on it? The question is factorable. Infant core-knowledge results --- object permanence, contact causality --- already lean partly innate \cite{spelke2007core}, so the design below should be read as discriminating \emph{beyond} the core-knowledge layer: what it factors is not whether structure is innate but which \emph{metaphysical reading} of that structure is, if any. Take the tree model of §\ref{sec:shades} seriously and suppose that intake of societal knowledge is mediated by the very capacities the dominant branch is strongest in; then populations differing in that mediation constitute natural comparison groups, and four suggest themselves for maximal differentiation: adults of the dominant cognitive branch in a Western society; adults of other branches, in their subcategories; infant children younger than two; and people of indigenous or otherwise non-Western societies. Two variables separate under such a comparison --- a sample's mindset, and the innate human mindset --- and the design is stated here as a proposal, not a result.

One reform of my own earlier conjecture belongs on the record, because the orangutan argument of §\ref{sec:shades} forced it. I had earlier supposed that a highly developed theory of mind might conflict with a generative intuition for the framework's way of thinking; but if the theory-of-mind construct \cite{premack1978does} dissolves under localize-the-flex --- if what apes lack is not a faculty but a predictable contextual environment --- then the conflict must be relocated accordingly: it is very directly the \emph{cultural implications of language itself}, the standing lexicon of §\ref{sec:shades}, that stands in tension with attaining the framework, not any faculty of mind-reading. On that reform, my own case reads as something of an optimization event --- a pre-linguistic mode of perception together with the articulation powers the dominant branch's language rewards, two modes usually in conflict\footnote{Usually, because otherwise someone would have discovered and propagated the framework ages ago --- and I remain genuinely hesitant to claim priority over one population in particular on exactly these grounds: deep thinkers comparatively immune to the dominant branch's entrenchment may have arrived here before me and simply never translated the result across the lexical boundary this paper describes. I state that as the open possibility it is, not as a finding.} --- and the hypothesis above is how the reading stops being about me: if the classical mindset is substantially socialized, then the population of minds positioned to see past it is a measurable feature of the world, and the prescription of §\ref{sec:buys} to attack the $\alpha$-space with artists by trade acquires a demography.

Prescriptions, stated as prescriptions --- tips of the Vonnegut species under §\ref{sec:art}'s line that heuristics are not axioms, not rules of the making. \emph{Keep genealogies.} The marginal cost of dated records is trivial --- a timestamp on a note --- and the discovery-theoretic value compounds;
