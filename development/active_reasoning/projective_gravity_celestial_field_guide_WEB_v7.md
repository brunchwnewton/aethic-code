# Projective Gravity — Version 7

### The Null Fiber: the arena as the primary's sky

> **What this version is.** Version 6 read in retarded time. The $u$-fibers are the primary's outgoing null rays; the level sets of $u$'s conjugate time are the primary's outgoing light cones — null hypersurfaces whose cross-sections are the 3-spheres; the spatial 3-sphere is the primary's *sky*, the sphere of null directions from its worldline; and the base $\mathbb{RP}^3$ is the space of unoriented null *lines* through the primary. Nothing in the physics of v6 changes. What changes is what the fiber, the sphere, and the base are *called* — and each name is a theorem about causal structure rather than a choice of coordinates.

> **What this version retracts.** Its own first draft. Read with $t$ as coordinate time, the null-fiber cover appeared to give instantaneous forward light, a one-sided sky, and closed null curves on $S^4$. All three were artifacts of treating retarded time as a synchronized clock. Read correctly, the cover is causal, the sky is two-sided, and $S^4$ is not excluded (§III).

# I. THE CONSTRUCTION

## I.1 The cover metric, and what it is [V]

$$g_{\rm cover} = -N^2dw^2 - 2A\,dw\,dr + r^2\,h$$

with $h$ the base's spatial metric lifted to the sphere. The $dr^2$ term of v6 is absent; $\partial_r$ is null. For $A = N = 1$ and $h$ round, substitute $w = t - r$:

$$-dw^2 - 2\,dw\,dr = -dt^2 + dr^2$$

**This is five-dimensional spacetime in Bondi coordinates.** The null fiber is the outgoing radial light ray at fixed retarded time. The construction is not a new metric; it is v6's cover with its time function chosen to be retarded time from the primary.

> ### ◆ LAW II′ — THE NULL FIBRATION
> The $u$-fibers are the generators of the primary's outgoing light cones: the null geodesics $\{w = {\rm const},\ \hat n = {\rm const}\}$. Their direction is v6's weaving law; their affine parameter is $r$. The base source $\int T\,dr$ along a generator is the null-line integral of matter.

## I.2 The level sets are null hypersurfaces [T]

The hypersurface $w = w_0$ is the primary's outgoing light cone emitted at retarded time $w_0$. Its induced metric is $r^2h$, degenerate along $\partial_r$: a null hypersurface, generated by the fibers, with 3-sphere cross-sections at each $r$. **The level 3-spheres are the wavefronts of the primary's light.** This is Entry 20's request — the 3-spheres null — realized in the only way a Lorentzian manifold permits: as cross-sections of null hypersurfaces, spacelike themselves, ruled by null generators.

## I.3 Spacelike from timelike and null [T/D]

Given a timelike $T$ and a null $K$ with $\alpha = -g(T,K)>0$, the vector $S = K - \alpha T$ has $g(S,S) = \alpha^2>0$ and $g(S,T) = 0$: **spacelike, orthogonal to $T$** — the spatial direction of the light ray in $T$'s rest frame. The null directions at a point of a 5-manifold, modulo scale, form the **celestial 3-sphere**, and $K\mapsto S$ maps it onto the unit sphere of spatial directions. So yes: a spacelike direction is recovered from a timelike one and a null one, and applied to every null direction at once it recovers the whole spatial sphere.

> **The spatial 3-sphere is the primary's sky** — the sphere of outgoing null directions from its worldline. The arena is not a slice of space that happens to be a sphere; it is what the primary sees.

## I.4 The base is the space of null lines [D]

A null line through the primary's worldline consists of two rays, $\pm\hat n$. The space of unoriented null lines is $S^3/\pm = \mathbb{RP}^3$. This is the base.

> **Gravity reads null lines through the primary; light reads null rays.** The seed's $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is line-versus-ray. Matter at $\hat n$ lies on the line $\{\hat n,-\hat n\}$; gravity, blind to which ray, sources the base at the line; the lift has wells at both rays; nothing is at $-\hat n$.

This is v6's seed with its objects named causally. The derivation of A2 — a great circle through the poles crosses the equator twice — becomes: a null line through the primary meets its sky twice.

# II. WHAT $A\neq N$ IS

Complete the square: $-N^2dw^2 - 2A\,dw\,dr = -N^2\big(dw + \tfrac{A}{N^2}dr\big)^2 + \tfrac{A^2}{N^2}dr^2$. With $t' = w + (A/N^2)r$,

$$g = -N^2dt'^2 + k^2dr^2 + r^2h, \qquad k = A/N$$

The spatial part $k^2dr^2 + r^2h$ with $\rho = kr$ is $d\rho^2 + (\rho/k)^2h$: **a cone with a solid-angle deficit of $1/k^3$ at the primary.** Locally light is isotropic — it is a metric — and the one parameter the null fiber introduces beyond v6 is a conical deficit at the source. $k = 1$ is v6 exactly. **[D]**

# IV. WHAT IS PRESERVED, AND WHY THE HOPF FIBER STAYS SPACELIKE

Everything in v6 is preserved: Law I on the base, the seed, the partition, Law III on the closed meridians of $S^4$, Law IV, the Weyl reading, the $G$ identity, the static wall. The cover is $S^4$ with the connected twin fiber, the forced poles, and Law III's automatic closure. Only the description has changed, and one parameter $k$ has been added.

**The Hopf fiber is not made null [D].** Nothing propagates along $E$'s circle — the photon is Hopf-neutral — so a null Hopf fiber constrains no signal. What it would do is destroy the fiber-length slot: a null direction has no length, and $L_E = \Omega L^{\rm ref}$ was the even datum that encoded Law I in the partition $3+1+2$. The count survives (a tilt $g_{w\psi}$ replaces the length), the encoding does not. It breaks the partition's meaning and buys nothing. The Hopf fiber stays spacelike.

# V. STATUS

**Standing.** Version 6, renamed by its causal structure, plus one parameter. Canonical alongside v6; the two are the same theory in different time functions.

**What the renaming buys [D].** Three statements that were definitions are now theorems about light: the arena is the primary's sky; the base is its space of null lines; the seed is line-versus-ray. And the $u$-position of a body — Entry 1's indexing — is its distance along the primary's light cone: **the fiber coordinate of a galaxy is how far the primary's light has travelled to reach it.** That is a more concrete meaning for $u$ than "the radial direction," and it makes the $u$-distribution of matter a statement about retarded distance from the center.

**Open [O].** What sets $k$. Whether a body's fiber coordinate — its retarded distance from the primary — is fixed or evolves, and what moves it.
