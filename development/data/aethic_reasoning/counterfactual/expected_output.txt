1. The four-attribute witness (Theorem: Cautious Monotony fails)
==============================================================================
A states s; closure: {s, y} | declared grounds of y: {'s'} | no other dependency edges
  blank set for x & y at evidential    : ['x', 'y']; intermediates: ['{}']
  blank set for x & y at unidirectional: ['x', 'y']; intermediates: ['{}']
  blank set for x & y at bidirectional : ['s', 'x', 'y']; intermediates: ['{}']
  reopen for x    : intermediates ['{s, y}']  (each blank on ['x']: True)
  pockets of pi_x    : ['(x, s, y, z)']
  reopen for x & y: intermediates ['{}']  (each blank on ['x', 'y']: True)
  pockets of pi_x & y: ['(x, ~s, y, ~z)', '(x, ~s, y, z)', '(x, s, y, z)']
  x |~ y : True | x |~ z : True | x&y |~ z : False | x |~ ~y : False
  => Cautious Monotony fails at (x, y, z) at every scope; Rational Monotony fails on the same instance.

2b. Cut without nesting (Proposition): priorities keep the generalization p over the particular r
==============================================================================
  declared grounds: y rests on p; x rests on p and r; the priority keeps the generalization p over the particular r
  reopening x, tie-keeping maxima: ['{p, y}', '{r, y}']
  priority selects: ['{p, y}'] -> pockets under x: ['(p, ~r, x, y)']
  reopening x & y: ['{r}'] -> pockets: ['(~p, r, x, y)']
  x |~ y: True | x&y |~ r: True | x |~ r: False (x |~ ~r: True)
  => Cut fails where the priorities select among inclusion-incomparable maximal restrictions.
     Branch-wise union: the two tied intermediates' restated contents are jointly inconsistent ({p, ~r, x, y} vs {~p, r, x, y}); their pockets are collected separately.

==============================================================================
  NORMALIZE=False: targets of x&(y|~y) = ['x', 'y'];  x&(y|~y) |~ z : False  (x |~ z : True)

  => un-normalized, the unrestricted conjunct reopens y and LLE fails; normalized, it contributes no target and LLE holds.

  => all three patterns fail, each by a witness: reopening d clears its ground c, so d |~ e and e |~ c do not chain.
